# Ethics, Justice & Civic Life

This domain file is part of the standards tree. It declares the strands and
grade-level objectives for **Ethics, Justice & Civic Life**. Only Kindergarten
objectives are filled in; the remaining grades (01–12) are authored by the agent loop
in Phase 1 (see [`docs/roadmap.md`](../../../../docs/roadmap.md)).

## Strands

### D09.S1 — Moral Reasoning & Values

Asking what is right and why, and forming one's own values with care.

### D09.S2 — Fairness, Justice & Rights

Fairness, dignity, and rights — and noticing where they are honored or denied.

### D09.S3 — Civic Participation & Collective Action

Taking part in shared life and acting collectively for the common good.

### D09.S4 — Peace, Conflict & Restorative Practice

Understanding conflict without violence and repairing harm.

## Progression (spiral)

In Kindergarten, ethics is sharing, taking turns, and kindness. By Grade 12, learners
reason through real dilemmas of justice, rights, and power, and practice the habits of
citizens who can disagree, repair, and build a fairer common life — with inquiry, not
creed, as their guide.
