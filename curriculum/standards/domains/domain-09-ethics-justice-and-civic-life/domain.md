# Ethics, Justice & Civic Life

This domain file is part of the standards tree. It declares the strands and the full
K–12 grade-level objectives for **Ethics, Justice & Civic Life**.

## Strands

### D09.S1 — Moral Reasoning & Values

Asking what is right and why, and forming one's own values with care.

### D09.S2 — Fairness, Justice & Rights

Fairness, dignity, and rights — and noticing where they are honored or denied.

### D09.S3 — Civic Participation & Collective Action

Taking part in shared life and acting collectively for the common good.

### D09.S4 — Peace, Conflict & Restorative Practice

Understanding conflict without violence and repairing harm.

## Progression (spiral)

In Kindergarten, ethics is sharing, taking turns, and kindness. In Grades 1–2,
learners name kind and unkind choices and give reasons, grasp that same treatment is
not always fair, help make class rules and decisions, and solve small conflicts with
words and real apologies. In Grades 3–5, they weigh reasons and values, tell needs
from wants, learn what rights are and where they come from, plan small collective
actions for the common good, and practice listening, "I" statements, and repairing
harm instead of only punishing. In Grades 6–8, they separate fact from belief from
value, meet real dilemmas and questions of justice and human rights, compare ways
societies make decisions (democracy and its alternatives), study social movements and
civil society, and apply restorative steps to real conflicts. In Grades 9–12, they
reason with multiple ethical frameworks, trace systemic injustice and critique human
rights across cultures, design collective action and weigh civic technology and
digital citizenship, and practice reconciliation, peacebuilding, and nonviolence. By
Grade 12, learners reason through real dilemmas of justice, rights, and power, and
practice the habits of citizens who can disagree, repair, and build a fairer common
life — with inquiry, not creed, as their guide.
