# Self & Identity

This document is an **exemplar** for the standards tree. It shows how a domain
declares its strands and grade-level objectives. Only Kindergarten objectives are
filled in; the remaining grades (01–12) are authored by the agent loop in Phase 1
(see [`docs/roadmap.md`](../../../docs/roadmap.md)).

## Strands

### D01.S1 — Emotional Literacy

Naming, expressing, and understanding feelings — one's own and others'.

### D01.S2 — Identity & Self-Concept

Developing a coherent, positive sense of self and belonging.

### D01.S3 — Self-Regulation & Agency

Managing impulses and acting as the author of one's own life.

## Progression (spiral)

The objectives spiral from naming feelings (K) toward nuanced emotional
understanding, identity in a global context, and self-directed agency (12). Each
grade builds on the previous.
