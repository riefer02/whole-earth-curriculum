# Self & Identity

This document is the **exemplar** for the standards tree. It shows how a domain
declares its strands and grade-level objectives. The full vertical ladder for
grades K–12 is declared here, so every lesson can trace to a standard
(see [`docs/roadmap.md`](../../../docs/roadmap.md)).

## Strands

### D01.S1 — Emotional Literacy

Naming, expressing, and understanding feelings — one's own and others'.

### D01.S2 — Identity & Self-Concept

Developing a coherent, positive sense of self and belonging.

### D01.S3 — Self-Regulation & Agency

Managing impulses and acting as the author of one's own life.

## Progression (spiral)

Each strand spirals from Kindergarten to Grade 12. **Emotional Literacy** moves
from naming and connecting basic feelings (K–2), through intensity, mixed and
complex emotions, and cultural expression of feeling (3–7), to analyzing how
emotion informs and distorts judgment and responding with emotional wisdom (8–12).
**Identity & Self-Concept** grows from sharing what makes one unique (K) through
values, multiple and intersecting identities, and a coherent personal narrative
(1–9) to authoring an integrated self within global, cultural, and technological
forces (10–12). **Self-Regulation & Agency** begins in Grade 1 with calming and
pausing, and rises through goal-setting, decision-making, and self-directed
learning (2–10) to full authorship of a meaningful life (11–12). Every grade
builds on the previous with no leaps.
