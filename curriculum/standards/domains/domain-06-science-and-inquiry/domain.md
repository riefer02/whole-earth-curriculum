# Science & Inquiry

This is an **exemplar** domain (alongside D01). It shows how a domain declares its
strands and grade-level objectives, and — crucially — how we hold the big questions
of origins as *stories grounded in evidence*, not as dogma (see
[`docs/philosophy.md`](../../../docs/philosophy.md) §11).

## Strands

- **D06.S1 — Scientific Inquiry:** asking, observing, predicting, testing, weighing
  evidence.
- **D06.S2 — Living Systems:** life, ecosystems, interdependence.
- **D06.S3 — Physical Systems:** matter, energy, forces.
- **D06.S4 — Earth, Space & Origins:** Earth, cosmos, and the stories of beginnings.

## Progression (spiral)

In Kindergarten, origins are a gentle story to wonder at. By Grade 12, learners weigh
cosmological models, geological evidence, and the relationship between scientific and
other ways of knowing — with the same posture of open inquiry.
