# Body & Wellness

This domain file is part of the standards tree. It declares the strands and
grade-level objectives for **Body & Wellness**. Only Kindergarten objectives are
filled in; the remaining grades (01–12) are authored by the agent loop in Phase 1
(see [`docs/roadmap.md`](../../../../docs/roadmap.md)).

## Strands

### D03.S1 — Body Awareness & the Senses

Knowing one's body, how it senses, and the signals it sends.

### D03.S2 — Movement & Motor Skills

Big and small movement, coordination, balance, and the joy of motion.

### D03.S3 — Health & Nutrition

Food, rest, and everyday habits that keep a body well.

### D03.S4 — Safety & Self-Care

Keeping oneself safe, caring for one's body, and knowing when to ask for help.

## Progression (spiral)

In Kindergarten, the body is explored through the senses, movement, and simple
self-care routines. By Grade 12, learners understand the deep connections among
body, emotion, and thought — and how to sustain their own health and the health of
others across a lifetime.
