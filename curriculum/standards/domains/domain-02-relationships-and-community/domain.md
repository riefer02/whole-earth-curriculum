# Relationships & Community

This domain file is part of the standards tree. It declares the strands and
grade-level objectives for **Relationships & Community**. Only Kindergarten
objectives are filled in; the remaining grades (01–12) are authored by the agent
loop in Phase 1 (see [`docs/roadmap.md`](../../../../docs/roadmap.md)).

## Strands

### D02.S1 — Interpersonal Skills

Communicating, cooperating, resolving small conflicts, and building healthy
relationships.

### D02.S2 — Empathy & Perspective-Taking

Sensing what others feel and think, and seeing a situation from another's point of
view.

### D02.S3 — Community & Belonging

Understanding the groups one belongs to and how communities are built and sustained.

### D02.S4 — Care, Cooperation & Mutual Aid

Helping others, sharing resources, and acting for the common good.

## Progression (spiral)

In Kindergarten, relationship is practiced through sharing, taking turns, and simple
kindness. By Grade 12, learners engage deep questions of belonging, solidarity, and
collective responsibility — from intimate friendship to global community — with the
same posture of care for the whole.
