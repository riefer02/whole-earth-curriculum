# Language & Communication

This domain file is part of the standards tree. It declares the strands and
grade-level objectives for **Language & Communication**. Only Kindergarten
objectives are filled in; the remaining grades (01–12) are authored by the agent
loop in Phase 1 (see [`docs/roadmap.md`](../../../../docs/roadmap.md)).

## Strands

### D04.S1 — Listening & Speaking

Listening with attention and speaking to be understood.

### D04.S2 — Reading & Literacy

Decoding and making meaning from written language.

### D04.S3 — Writing & Expression

Expressing ideas, stories, and feelings through written marks.

### D04.S4 — Multilingual & Intercultural Communication

Valuing many languages and communicating with respect across cultures.

## Progression (spiral)

In Kindergarten, language grows through listening, retelling, and play with letters,
sounds, and marks. By Grade 12, learners read, write, and reason across languages and
registers, able to hear many voices and speak with clarity and care in a multilingual
world.
