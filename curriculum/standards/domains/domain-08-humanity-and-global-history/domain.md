# Humanity & Global History

This domain file is part of the standards tree. It declares the strands and
grade-level objectives for **Humanity & Global History**. Only Kindergarten objectives
are filled in; the remaining grades (01–12) are authored by the agent loop in Phase 1
(see [`docs/roadmap.md`](../../../../docs/roadmap.md)).

## Strands

### D08.S1 — Time, Change & Continuity

Time, sequence, and how things change or stay the same.

### D08.S2 — World Cultures & Knowledge Traditions

The many ways peoples have lived, known, and made meaning.

### D08.S3 — Human Geography & Migration

How people are shaped by place and how they move across the Earth.

### D08.S4 — Power, Resistance & Liberation

How power has been held and contested, and the struggles for freedom and dignity.

## Progression (spiral)

In Kindergarten, history is "then and now" through stories and artifacts — gentle,
personal, and held with care. By Grade 12, learners weigh competing accounts, trace
global movements of peoples and power, and understand their own place in the long,
unfinished human story.
