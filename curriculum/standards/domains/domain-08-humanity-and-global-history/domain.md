# Humanity & Global History

This domain file is part of the standards tree. It declares the strands and the full
K–12 grade-level objectives for **Humanity & Global History**.

## Strands

### D08.S1 — Time, Change & Continuity

Time, sequence, and how things change or stay the same.

### D08.S2 — World Cultures & Knowledge Traditions

The many ways peoples have lived, known, and made meaning.

### D08.S3 — Human Geography & Migration

How people are shaped by place and how they move across the Earth.

### D08.S4 — Power, Resistance & Liberation

How power has been held and contested, and the struggles for freedom and dignity.

## Progression (spiral)

In Kindergarten, history is "then and now" through stories and artifacts — gentle,
personal, and held with care. In Grades 1–5, learners sequence their own and their
community's past on timelines, meet the daily lives, traditions, and knowledge of many
peoples, and explore why people live where they do and why they move, while fairness
and dignity are explored through stories of helping, speaking up, and peaceful
resistance. In Grades 6–8, they begin to work with primary and secondary sources,
compare calendars and knowledge systems, trace migrations (voluntary and forced) and
trade networks, and engage — with care — the history of colonization and slavery and
the resistance of enslaved and colonized peoples. In Grades 9–12, they analyze
continuity and change across global processes, honor and weigh many knowledge
traditions side by side, trace the transatlantic slave trade and modern displacement,
and study empire, liberation movements, and the remembrance of genocide and mass
violence. By Grade 12, learners weigh competing accounts, trace global movements of
peoples and power, and understand their own place in the long, unfinished human story.
