# Creative & Aesthetic Expression

This domain file is part of the standards tree. It declares the strands and
grade-level objectives for **Creative & Aesthetic Expression**, spanning Kindergarten
through Grade 12.

## Strands

### D07.S1 — Visual Arts

Making and responding to visual work and reading the images around us.

### D07.S2 — Music & Sound

Making, listening to, and understanding music and sound.

### D07.S3 — Movement, Dance & Drama

Expressing meaning and feeling through the moving body.

### D07.S4 — Making & Design

Imagining, planning, and building things with purpose and care.

## Progression (spiral)

In Kindergarten, making is the whole curriculum: mark-making, song, pretend, and
building with whatever is at hand. In the early grades (1–3) learners gain the basic
elements of each strand — line, shape, and color; steady beat and pitch; movement and
character; planning and testing — and begin to describe and compare what they make.
In the middle grades (4–5) they learn craft (notation, depth, dance forms, a real
design cycle) and meet the arts of other cultures as living traditions to be honored,
not specimens. In grades 6–8 formal reasoning emerges, so learners compose, devise,
and prototype with intention, then critique their own and others' work using evidence,
separating observation from judgment. In high school (9–12) they wield technology and
craft deliberately, examine the ethics of cultural engagement and of sustainable,
accessible design, and finally create and critique with skill and insight, honoring
the arts of many cultures and using their own creative voice for joy, meaning, and
change. Every grade builds on the one before with no leaps.
