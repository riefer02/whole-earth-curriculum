# Mathematics & Reasoning

This domain file is part of the standards tree. It declares the strands and
grade-level objectives for **Mathematics & Reasoning**. Only Kindergarten objectives
are filled in; the remaining grades (01–12) are authored by the agent loop in Phase 1
(see [`docs/roadmap.md`](../../../../docs/roadmap.md)).

## Strands

### D05.S1 — Number & Quantity

Counting, comparing, and operating with numbers in meaningful contexts.

### D05.S2 — Patterns & Relationships

Seeing, making, and generalizing patterns and relationships.

### D05.S3 — Geometry & Spatial Reasoning

Shape, space, position, and how objects fit and move.

### D05.S4 — Data, Measurement & Probability

Measuring, making sense of data, and reasoning about chance.

## Progression (spiral)

In Kindergarten, mathematics is embodied: counting real objects, clapping patterns,
and naming shapes in the world. By Grade 12, learners reason with abstraction,
model systems, and weigh uncertainty — always grounded in the sense-making that began
in the sandbox and the circle.
