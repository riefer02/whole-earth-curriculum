# Mathematics & Reasoning

This domain file is part of the standards tree. It declares the strands and the
full vertical ladder of grade-level objectives (K–12) for **Mathematics & Reasoning**,
so every lesson can trace to a standard (see
[`docs/roadmap.md`](../../../../docs/roadmap.md)).

## Strands

### D05.S1 — Number & Quantity

Counting, comparing, and operating with numbers in meaningful contexts.

### D05.S2 — Patterns & Relationships

Seeing, making, and generalizing patterns and relationships.

### D05.S3 — Geometry & Spatial Reasoning

Shape, space, position, and how objects fit and move.

### D05.S4 — Data, Measurement & Probability

Measuring, making sense of data, and reasoning about chance.

## Progression (spiral)

Each strand spirals from Kindergarten to Grade 12 with no gaps or leaps. **Number &
Quantity** moves from counting and comparing real objects (K) through place value and
the four operations (1–4), fractions, decimals, ratios, and rational and irrational
numbers (5–8), to real and complex numbers, precision, and quantitative reasoning
about large systems (9–12). **Patterns & Relationships** grows from copying repeating
patterns (K) through growing patterns, simple equations, variables, and functions
(1–8) to modeling linear, exponential, periodic, and algorithmic relationships
(9–12). **Geometry & Spatial Reasoning** begins with naming shapes and position words
(K), then builds through attributes, area, perimeter, volume, and the coordinate
plane (1–6) to transformations, the Pythagorean theorem, trigonometry, and design
reasoning (7–12). **Data, Measurement & Probability** starts with sorting and grouping
(K), then measures, graphs, and summarizes data (1–5), and rises through variability,
sampling, probability, and inference (6–12) to weighing uncertainty and detecting
misleading statistics and algorithmic bias. Every grade deepens the sense-making
that began in the sandbox and the circle, never jumping ahead.
