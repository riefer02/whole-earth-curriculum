# Technology, Media & Environment

This domain file is part of the standards tree. It declares the strands and
grade-level objectives for **Technology, Media & Environment**. Only Kindergarten
objectives are filled in; the remaining grades (01–12) are authored by the agent loop
in Phase 1 (see [`docs/roadmap.md`](../../../../docs/roadmap.md)).

## Strands

### D10.S1 — Technology & Society

Tools and technologies and how they change human life.

### D10.S2 — Media & Information Literacy

Reading media critically and creating it responsibly.

### D10.S3 — Ecology & Environmental Systems

Living systems and the interdependence of life and environment.

### D10.S4 — Sustainability & Stewardship

Caring for the Earth so all life can flourish.

## Progression (spiral)

In Kindergarten, children meet technology as helpful tools, media as story to be
questioned, and the Earth as a home to care for. By Grade 12, learners analyze the
deep effects of technology and media on humanity and the planet, and act as informed
stewards of the living systems that sustain all of us.
