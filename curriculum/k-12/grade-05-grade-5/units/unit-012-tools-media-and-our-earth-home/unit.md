# Unit 12 — Tools, Media & Our Earth Home

## Overview

This unit closes the year by gathering everything together, building on Grade 4's
idea of design, its ecosystems, and reduce-reuse-recycle. Learners explain how a
technology people use every day, such as a phone or a water pump, has changed daily
life over time, and compare how people in two different places in the world use a
similar technology differently. They evaluate whether a claim is likely true by
checking more than one source and noting where they agree or disagree, and recognize
when a message favors one side and what fair reporting would include. They trace the
path of energy or matter, such as water, through a simple cycle or food web, and
explain how living things in an ecosystem depend on one another and what can happen
when one part changes. They describe a form of pollution or habitat loss and
something people can do to reduce it, and explain what it means to use a resource
carefully so it lasts. The unit ends in a celebration of each whole person, the
caring community, and the living Earth — and looks ahead to Grade 6.

## Essential questions

- How do tools and media change our lives, and how do I read them critically?
- How does matter and energy cycle through the living world, and how do I care for it?
