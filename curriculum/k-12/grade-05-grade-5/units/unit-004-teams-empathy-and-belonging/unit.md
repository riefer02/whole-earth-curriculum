# Unit 4 — Teams, Empathy & Belonging

## Overview

This unit turns outward into the practice of care, building on Grade 4's active
listening and fair negotiation. Learners give and receive feedback kindly and use it
to improve shared work, and cooperate in a team by dividing tasks fairly and
following through on their part. They take the perspective of a person from a
different culture or place and describe their daily life, and recognize when a first
guess about someone's feelings might be wrong — and check with them. They explain how
communities are built through shared work, symbols, and stories, investigate how
people in a community meet shared needs such as food, safety, and learning, describe
examples of mutual aid from different cultures and times, and practice fairness by
dividing resources so everyone has enough, in a role-play or real task.

## Essential questions

- How do we give kind feedback and work as a fair team?
- How do we see from another's point of view and build belonging together?
