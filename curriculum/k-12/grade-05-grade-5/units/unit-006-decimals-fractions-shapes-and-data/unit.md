# Unit 6 — Decimals, Fractions, Shapes & Data

## Overview

This unit is the heart of the year's numeracy work, building on Grade 4's place value
to 1,000,000 and multi-digit operations. Learners read, write, compare, and round
decimals to the thousandths and connect them to fractions with denominators of 10 and
100, and add, subtract, multiply, and divide fractions and decimals in real contexts
such as sharing, cooking, and money. They plot points and graph relationships in the
first quadrant of a coordinate grid, write and evaluate simple expressions and
interpret what they mean, graph points and use them to solve problems about location
and distance, find the volume of right rectangular prisms using unit cubes and
formulas, convert among different-sized standard measurement units, and represent and
interpret data on line plots with fractions. Mathematics is sense-making with real
things, drawn from many traditions, for everyone.

## Essential questions

- How do decimals, fractions, and expressions help me make sense of quantities?
- How do coordinates, volume, measurement, and data help me make sense of space and information?
