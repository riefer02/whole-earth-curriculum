# Unit 1 — How Do We Know?

## Overview

This unit sets the pace for middle-grade science. It opens with a belief most learners
already hold — heavy things sink — and tests it. From there the class takes one skill at
a time: sharpening a wonder into a testable question, deciding what counts as evidence,
separating observation from inference, and spotting an opinion dressed as evidence.

The second half is one investigation spine. Learners design a fair test of paper
absorbency, run it with repeated trials, meet an anomaly in their own data, check a
manufacturer's claim against it, and finish by running an investigation of their own and
defending a claim–evidence–reasoning paragraph. Concrete throughout, but genuinely
rigorous.

## Essential questions

- How do we know what we know?
- What is the difference between seeing something happen and explaining why it happens?
