# Unit 8 — Making, Music & Movement

## Overview

This unit gives craft to the imagination, building on Grade 4's depth and space,
basic notation, dance forms, and the design cycle. Learners plan and complete an
artwork with a clear idea, choosing materials and techniques to fit the idea, and
explain how an artwork from a specific culture reflects that culture's place,
materials, or beliefs, citing what they observe. They compose a short piece or song
with a clear beginning, middle, and end and perform it, and explain how music
reflects the place, history, or community that made it, citing what they hear. They
choreograph a short group dance or scene with a clear idea, working cooperatively,
and explain how dance or drama reflects the place, history, or community that made
it. They design and build something that solves a real problem in their class or
community, and compare designs made for different climates or resources, explaining
how the environment shapes design. The arts of other cultures are met as living
traditions to honor, never as specimens.

## Essential questions

- How do I plan and carry out an artwork, song, or scene with a clear idea?
- How do the arts reflect the place, history, and community that made them?
