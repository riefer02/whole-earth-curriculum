# Unit 3 — My Body, My Movement & My Growth

## Overview

This unit reads the body with new precision, building on Grade 4's sense of how the
body changes with activity and rest. Learners explain the mind–body connection with a
simple example, such as how worry can make a stomach feel tight, and describe how
bodies grow and change at different rates — and that every pace is normal. They refine
a specific motor skill, such as throwing accurately or balancing, through repeated
practice and give a peer helpful feedback, and explain how regular movement benefits
the heart, muscles, and mood. The wide range of human bodies and abilities is honored
with dignity and joy, never shame.

## Essential questions

- How are my mind and my body connected, and how do I grow?
- How does movement help my body, and how do I get better at moving?
