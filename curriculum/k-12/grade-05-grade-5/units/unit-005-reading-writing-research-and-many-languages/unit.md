# Unit 5 — Reading, Writing, Research & Many Languages

## Overview

This unit is the heart of the year's literacy work, building on Grade 4's fluency,
comprehension, and the plan–draft–revise–edit–publish process. Learners paraphrase a
speaker's points accurately and ask clarifying questions when unsure, and give a
short presentation with a clear main idea, supporting reasons, and a conclusion. They
explain how an author uses structure, such as cause and effect or problem and
solution, to organize ideas, and distinguish fact from opinion and identify the
author's purpose and point of view. They write for different purposes and audiences,
choosing an appropriate style and structure, and gather information from several
sources, note key facts, and write a short report in their own words. They recognize
that many writing systems exist, such as alphabets and character scripts, and
identify a few, and describe how language is tied to culture, such as words for
family, food, or respect that differ across languages. Home language is honored
first, and multilingualism is treated as wealth.

## Essential questions

- How do I understand, evaluate, and share what I read and hear?
- How do I research, write for a purpose, and honor the many languages people speak?
