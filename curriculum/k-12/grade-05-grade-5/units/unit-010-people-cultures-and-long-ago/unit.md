# Unit 10 — People, Cultures & Long Ago

## Overview

This unit steps into the wider human story, building on Grade 4's dated timelines and
knowledge traditions. Learners build a timeline that compares two cultures or
civilizations and identify what changed and what stayed the same in each, and explain
how a tool or technology, such as writing, the wheel, or the plow, changed how people
lived and worked. They compare origin stories from two or more peoples and explain
how each reflects the people who tell it, and describe an achievement in art,
building, or learning from a non-European civilization and what it shows about that
society. They trace the migration of a specific people and explain the push and pull
reasons for their movement, and explain how trade and travel connected peoples across
land and sea and what moved with them. They describe a historical example of people
treated unfairly who resisted or helped one another, and explain what fairness and
dignity mean using a concrete example of when they were honored or denied. History is
told as a global story from many perspectives, never one people's triumph.

## Essential questions

- How do timelines and tools show change and continuity across civilizations?
- How have peoples lived, known, moved, traded, and stood for fairness and dignity?
