# Unit 7 — Food Webs, Forces, Earth & Sky

## Overview

This unit deepens the science the year opened with, building on Grade 4's controlled
experiments and the living world. Learners explain how matter and energy move through
a food web and what can happen when one part changes, and describe how traits pass
from parents to offspring and how some traits vary within a family. They explain that
matter is made of particles too small to see and use this idea to explain changes of
state, and describe gravity and magnetism as forces that act at a distance. They
describe the Earth, Moon, and Sun system and explain day length, moon phases, and
eclipses, and explain how Earth's surface is changed by slow and fast processes, and
where earthquakes and volcanoes occur. Throughout, the "how do we know" through-line
holds: predict, test, observe, and separate evidence from inference.

## Essential questions

- How does matter and energy move through the living world?
- What forces and changes shape matter, Earth, and the sky?
