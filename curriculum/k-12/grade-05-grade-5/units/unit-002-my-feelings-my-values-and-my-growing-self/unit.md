# Unit 2 — My Feelings, My Values & My Growing Self

## Overview

This unit opens the year inward, building on Grade 4's work of describing feeling
intensity and complex feelings such as jealousy, guilt, and pride. Learners now
explain how thoughts, memories, and body sensations can shape a feeling, and compare
how feelings are expressed in different families, cultures, and situations. They name
the values they care about — fairness, honesty, kindness — and give an example of
living by one, and explain how a person's identity is shaped by family, culture,
place, and history. They monitor their own behavior and feelings during a task and
name what helped or hurt their focus, and take responsibility for a choice and its
effects on others, naming a better next step. Every family's shape and every child's
pace are honored.

## Essential questions

- How do my thoughts, memories, and body shape what I feel?
- What do I value, and what makes me who I am becoming?
