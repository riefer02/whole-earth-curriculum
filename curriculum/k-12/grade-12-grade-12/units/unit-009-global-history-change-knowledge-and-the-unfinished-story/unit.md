# Unit 9 — Global History: Change, Knowledge & the Unfinished Story

## Overview

This unit gives Grade 12 its culminating pass through the long, many-voiced human
story, deepening Grade 11's hardest and most careful questions. Learners weigh
competing accounts of change and continuity across global history and form a reasoned
synthesis of the evidence, and reflect on their own place in the long, unfinished
human story, connecting the past to the present and to possible futures. They weigh
multiple knowledge traditions side by side and articulate a respectful, non-dogmatic
relationship among them, and reflect on how honoring many knowledge traditions
contributes to a more just and flourishing shared human story. They trace global
movements of peoples and power across deep time and synthesize how migration has
shaped the human story, and reflect on their own place in the ongoing movement of
people and ideas and their responsibility to a shared and changing Earth. They weigh
competing accounts of power and resistance in a contested historical event and form a
reasoned judgment, and reflect on their own role and responsibility in the ongoing,
unfinished struggle for freedom and dignity for all people. History is told from many
perspectives, never as a single nation's triumph, and the voices of those too often
left out are centered with care and accuracy.

## Essential questions

- How do I weigh competing accounts of change and continuity and form a reasoned synthesis, and where is my own place in the long, unfinished human story?
- How do I weigh multiple knowledge traditions side by side and honor them without dogma, and trace global movements of peoples and power across deep time?
- How do I weigh competing accounts of power and resistance, and reflect on my own responsibility in the unfinished struggle for freedom and dignity?
