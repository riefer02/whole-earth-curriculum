# Unit 8 — Health, Nutrition & Care

## Overview

This unit returns to the body at the threshold of adult life, now with independence
and care for others. Learners synthesize the science of nutrition with ethical,
cultural, and environmental considerations to make lifelong food decisions, and
advocate for food systems and policies that support health and dignity for all people,
linking individual and collective wellbeing. They manage their own health
independently, integrating sleep, movement, nutrition, mental health, and medical
care, and explain how to care for the health and safety of others, including basic
emergency response and caregiving, and when to step in. Every body, every pace, and
every family's way of living is honored; the body is taught with dignity, never shame.

## Essential questions

- How do I synthesize the science of nutrition with ethical, cultural, and environmental considerations to make lifelong food decisions?
- How do I advocate for food systems and policies that support health and dignity for all people, linking individual and collective wellbeing?
- How do I manage my own health independently, and how do I care for the health and safety of others — including emergency response and caregiving?
