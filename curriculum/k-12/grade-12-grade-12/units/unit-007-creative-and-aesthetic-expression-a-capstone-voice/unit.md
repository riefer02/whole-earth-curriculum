# Unit 7 — Creative & Aesthetic Expression: A Capstone Voice

## Overview

This unit gives the co-author's deliberate, ethical voice its mature form: a
sustained, coherent body of work and a critically discerning eye. Learners produce a
sustained body of artwork that demonstrates technical skill and a coherent personal
vision, presented with a written artist statement, and critique artwork across
cultures and eras with insight, honoring diverse traditions while forming and
defending an independent judgment. They produce and perform or present a sustained
body of musical work that shows skill and a coherent personal voice, with a written
statement, and critique music across cultures and eras with insight. They produce and
perform a sustained piece that shows technical skill and a coherent creative vision,
with a written artist statement, and critique performance across cultures and eras
with insight. They design and realize a substantial project that demonstrates craft
and a coherent purpose, presented with a written rationale, and evaluate design across
cultures and eras with insight, honoring diverse traditions while forming and
defending an independent judgment about good design. The arts of other cultures are
met as living traditions to honor with respectful questions, never as specimens.

## Essential questions

- How do I produce a sustained body of artwork, music, and performance that shows skill and a coherent personal vision, and present it with a written statement?
- How do I critique art, music, performance, and design across cultures and eras with insight, honoring diverse traditions while forming and defending an independent judgment?
- How do I design and realize a substantial project that demonstrates craft and a coherent purpose, and justify good design across cultures and eras?
