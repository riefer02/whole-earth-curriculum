# Unit 11 — Technology, Media & the Living Earth

## Overview

This unit gathers the outward year into stewardship — neither worship nor fear.
Learners analyze technology as a system shaping society, weighing design, governance,
and their own choices, and argue for a humane direction, and reflect on their own
relationship with technology — what it gives and what it costs — and how they will use
it as a steward rather than a consumer. They practice responsible media creation by
making and sharing a message that is accurate, fair, and honest about its sources, and
reflect on how they will stay informed in a flood of information, holding claims open
to evidence rather than settling into certainty. They explain Earth as one
interconnected system and how human activity is pushing against the planetary
boundaries that sustain all life, and reflect on their place within the living world
and how understanding interdependence shapes how they choose to live. They design and
defend a stewardship plan for a real place or resource, weighing fairness to present
and future generations, and reflect on their own responsibility as a steward of the
Earth and how they will act — individually and with others — for all life.

## Essential questions

- How do I analyze technology as a system shaping society, and how will I use it as a steward rather than a consumer?
- How do I create media responsibly and stay informed in a flood of information, holding claims open to evidence?
- How do I explain Earth as one interconnected system and design — and defend — a stewardship plan for a real place or resource?
