# Unit 1 — Feelings, Identity & Purpose

## Overview

This unit opens the capstone year inward, building on Grade 11's articulated-and-
committed purpose. Learners now *integrate* their feelings, identity, and purpose into
a single, stable sense of who they are. They integrate emotional understanding into
ethical judgment, recognizing how emotion informs and can bias moral reasoning, and
respond to complex, emotionally charged personal and global situations with insight,
care, and emotional wisdom. They articulate an integrated sense of who they are, what
they value, and how they will act, held together across contexts, and explain how they
author their identity within global, cultural, and technological forces rather than
being defined by them. They exercise self-directed agency by setting, planning, and
acting on a meaningful life goal with autonomy and accountability, and sustain
self-regulation and purpose through setbacks and systemic constraints, naming what
sustains them. Every self is honored as a whole in the making, never a label, and
every question is held as a question.

## Essential questions

- How does emotion inform and bias moral reasoning, and how do I respond to emotionally charged personal and global situations with insight and care?
- Who am I — across contexts and within global, cultural, and technological forces — and how do I author, rather than merely inherit, my identity?
- How do I set and act on a meaningful life goal with autonomy and accountability, and what sustains my purpose through setbacks and systemic constraints?
