# Unit 4 — Language, Literature & Deliberation

## Overview

This unit grows from Grade 11's multilingual, critical, and persuasive voice into
deliberation, integration, and language justice. Learners engage in complex, sustained
deliberation on contested issues, hearing many voices and reaching reasoned positions,
and speak with clarity, care, and integrity in public and professional settings,
adapting to diverse audiences and purposes. They read, evaluate, and integrate
scholarly, technical, and literary texts across registers and disciplines, and reason
across multiple writing systems and languages, recognizing how each encodes meaning
differently. They produce a sustained, polished piece of writing that demonstrates
command of structure, style, and evidence for a chosen audience, and write with
clarity and care across disciplines and registers, using language ethically and
precisely in a multilingual world. They advocate for language justice, making
communication accessible to people of all languages in public life, and move
confidently and respectfully across languages and cultures. Home language is honored
first, and multilingualism is held as wealth.

## Essential questions

- How do I deliberate on contested issues, hearing many voices and reaching reasoned positions, and speak with clarity, care, and integrity in public and professional settings?
- How do I read, evaluate, and integrate scholarly, technical, and literary texts — and reason across multiple writing systems and languages?
- How do I write a sustained, polished piece for a chosen audience, and advocate for language justice in a multilingual world?
