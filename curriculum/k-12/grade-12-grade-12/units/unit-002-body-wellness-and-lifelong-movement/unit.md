# Unit 2 — Body, Wellness & Lifelong Movement

## Overview

This unit reads the body as the somatic ground of thought and feeling, now integrated
into a lifelong framework and understood as a shared public concern. Learners
integrate the body–mind connection into a personal framework for lifelong awareness
and wellbeing and explain it to others, and articulate how to sustain their own and
others' embodied health and dignity across a lifetime, including aging and change.
They integrate movement into a lifelong, adaptable plan that supports physical,
mental, and social health, and explain how environments — cities, workplaces, natural
spaces — shape people's ability to move and propose changes that widen access. The
body is taught with dignity and joy, honoring the wide range of bodies and abilities
and the whole arc of a life.

## Essential questions

- How do I integrate the body–mind connection into a personal framework for lifelong awareness and wellbeing, and explain it to others?
- How do I sustain my own and others' embodied health and dignity across a lifetime, including aging and change?
- How do I integrate movement into a lifelong, adaptable plan, and how do environments shape — and widen or narrow — people's ability to move?
