# Unit 3 — Relationships, Community & Collective Care

## Overview

This unit turns outward into care at its most mature — intimate trust, integrated
empathy, and collective responsibility for the whole living world. Learners sustain
intimate, trusting relationships through honest communication, repair, and mutual
respect, and reflect on their own communication and relationship habits, committing to
grow as a listener and partner. They integrate empathy and perspective-taking into
ethical reasoning about complex social problems, and act on empathy to reduce
suffering and promote dignity for others, including the more-than-human world. They
articulate their own place within nested communities, from family to the global
community of all life, and weigh belonging, solidarity, and collective responsibility
in deciding how to act for the common good. They articulate a personal ethic of care,
cooperation, and mutual aid for a just and thriving world, and act on collective
responsibility for people and the Earth, connecting local action to global solidarity.

## Essential questions

- How do I sustain intimate, trusting relationships through honest communication, repair, and mutual respect, and grow as a listener and partner?
- How do I integrate empathy into ethical reasoning about complex social problems and act on it — for people and for the more-than-human world?
- How do I weigh belonging, solidarity, and collective responsibility, and act on a personal ethic of care and mutual aid from local action to global solidarity?
