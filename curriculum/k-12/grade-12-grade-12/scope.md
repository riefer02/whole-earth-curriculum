# Grade 12 Scope

## How to read this scope

Each `unit_id` in the frontmatter resolves to a unit file under `units/`.
`start_day`/`end_day` map the unit onto the 180-day school year; the list is a
continuous sequence from day 1 to day 180 with no gaps or overlaps. `domain` and
`pillar` name the primary domain(s) and pillar(s) each unit serves. `domain_weighting`
expresses the intended balance of instructional time across the ten domains, in
percent of the year. `lesson_count` counts the core lessons; the days between lessons
carry flex time, assessment, integration, and celebration. `assessment_plan`
describes how a learner's growth is seen and recorded at this level.

## Year at a glance

The year runs in four movements: inward, consolidating the three things a learner
carries for life — an integrated self, a body understood as a lifelong practice, and
relationships that hold; then the heart of the year, given to the four great tools of
school at full maturity; then the full adult questions of the unfinished human story,
the just society, and the living Earth. Throughout, the purpose Grade 11 named and stood
for is *consolidated* into something a learner can act on.

*Feelings, Identity & Purpose* (U.12.001, D01) *integrates* Grade 11's committed purpose
into one stable self, holding emotion and judgment together and acting on a meaningful
life goal. *Body, Wellness & Lifelong Movement* (U.12.002, D03) carries the body into a
lifelong framework for wellbeing and a shared concern for how environments widen access
to movement. *Relationships, Community & Collective Care* (U.12.003, D02) turns outward
into care at its most mature: trust sustained through repair, empathy brought to complex
social problems, and mutual aid reaching from family to the whole living world.

*Language, Literature & Deliberation* (U.12.004, D04) grows into deliberation on
contested questions, the integration of scholarly and literary texts, a polished piece
for a chosen audience, and advocacy for language justice. *Mathematics: Reasoning about
Large Systems* (U.12.005, D05) reaches the summit of the ladder: limits and continuity,
quantitative reasoning about populations, energy, and carbon, matrices, and algorithms
judged for fairness. *Science: Systems, Evidence & Our Origins* (U.12.006, D06) carries
Grade 11's investigation into weighing competing explanations and the ethics of
knowledge: learners weigh ecosystems, economies, and health as coupled systems and hold
scientific accounts of life's origin alongside cultural accounts — evidence-grounded
among many, never dogma. *Creative & Aesthetic Expression: A Capstone Voice* (U.12.007,
D07) gives the co-author's voice its mature form: sustained work in art, music,
performance, and design, and a discerning critical eye on the arts of other cultures,
met as living traditions. *Health, Nutrition & Care* (U.12.008, D03) returns to the body
at the threshold of adult life: lifelong food decisions, advocacy for food systems that
support dignity for all, and health managed independently.

*Global History: Change, Knowledge & the Unfinished Story* (U.12.009, D08) makes the
culminating pass through the many-voiced human story, weighing competing accounts of
change and continuity and knowledge traditions side by side. History is told from many
perspectives, never one nation's triumph, and the voices too often left out are centered
with care. *Ethics, Justice & Civic Life* (U.12.010, D09) deepens Grade 11's justice and
solidarity into the habits of a citizen: moral dilemmas with no clean answer, contested
questions of justice defended with reasons, and peacemaking that repairs harm. Questions
are held as questions, never answered as dogma. *Technology, Media & the Living Earth*
(U.12.011, D10) gathers the outward year into stewardship, neither worship nor fear:
technology analyzed as a system shaping society, media made responsibly and read with
claims open to evidence, Earth explained as one interconnected system under pressure,
and a stewardship plan weighed for fairness to future generations.

The year closes in *All of Me, All of Us* (U.12.012, D01 + D02), gathering the
integrated self, the solidary community, and the living Earth into a considered life
direction, and handing each learner off to adult life as a whole person and a citizen.

Throughout, the four pillars are interwoven. Intellectual awareness (P3) carries the
most weight — the multilingual deliberative voice, the summit of quantitative and
algorithmic reasoning, the ethics of science, and the sustained creative voice.
Contextual and ecological awareness (P4) holds its large share, for Grade 12 faces the
deepest questions of the human story, the just society, and the living Earth, while
emotional and social awareness (P1) remains the ground and a capable body-and-self (P2)
is what all of it stands on. The hardest truths — inequality, ecological crisis,
injustice, moral ambiguity — are met honestly, with intellectual tools and agency, never
nihilism.
