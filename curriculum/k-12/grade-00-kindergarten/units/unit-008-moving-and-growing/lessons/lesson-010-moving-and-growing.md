# Lesson 10 — Moving & Growing

## Summary

This capstone lesson gathers everything from the unit. Learners celebrate that
movement, senses, and food all work together to help a body grow strong and happy,
revisit each idea at a celebration circuit, and create a "things that help me grow"
drawing to keep.

## Objectives

- Name the five senses and identify a body part used for each. _(D03.S1.K.01)_
- Demonstrate big movements (run, hop, balance) and small movements (draw, stack) with
  growing control. _(D03.S2.K.01)_
- Sort common foods into "everyday" and "sometimes" groups with guidance.
  _(D03.S3.K.01)_

## Connection

You are growing every single day — taller, stronger, and more able. Three things help
you grow together: moving your body, using your senses to enjoy the world, and eating
food that fuels you. You move to build strength, your senses help you notice and
delight in life, and food gives you the energy to do both. All of these are your body's
way of growing — and every body grows at its own pace, which is exactly right.

## Materials

- Crayons and paper
- Open space for a celebration circuit
- Food picture cards and sense cards (from earlier lessons)

## Preparation

- Set up three simple celebration stations: a movement spot, a senses spot, and a food
  spot.
- Gather food cards and sense cards from earlier lessons.
- Prepare space to hang the finished drawings together.

## Facilitator note

This lesson is written to the learner ("you"). As facilitator, make this a joyful,
low-pressure celebration — the unit's summative moment should feel like a party, not a
test. **Honor every body and every pace of growth:** remind learners that bodies grow
at different speeds and in different ways, and that every pace is worthy. Observe each
learner across the three stations to gather evidence of the unit's three objectives.
Keep the message gentle: no comparisons, no "best," just celebration of each learner's
own growing.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Stand or sit tall. Think about all you have done in this unit:
   moving big and small, sensing the world, and learning about food. Today we celebrate
   how they all help you grow.
2. **Celebration circuit (25 min).** Visit each station:
   - **Movement:** do one big movement (run, hop, or balance) and one small movement
     (draw a shape or stack a few things). Feel your body working.
   - **Senses:** point to each sensing body part and name the five senses. Share one
     thing your senses love.
   - **Food:** sort a few food cards into "everyday" and "sometimes," and name one food
     that helps you grow.
3. **Make your drawing (10 min).** Draw a picture of the things that help you grow:
   you moving, something your senses love, and a food that fuels you. Write or draw
   your name on it.
4. **Share and celebrate (5 min).** Hang the drawings together as one big class
   picture. Look at how many ways there are to move, sense, and grow.
5. **Close (5 min).** Put a hand on your heart and feel it beat — proof that your body
   is alive, working, and growing. What is one thing you are proud your body can do
   now?

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Visit one station at a time and allow adapted versions of every
  movement; let a learner dictate or describe their drawing while an adult or peer
  scribes.
- **Extension:** Invite learners to add a fourth "growth helper" of their own (rest,
  water, love) to their drawing and explain it.

## Assessment

- **Summative (portfolio):** The "things that help me grow" drawing, kept as a unit
  artifact showing movement, senses, and food.
- **Observation:** Across the three stations, can the learner demonstrate a movement,
  name the senses, and sort a food — the unit's three objectives?

## Home connection

Show your "things that help me grow" drawing to a grown-up and tell them the three
ways you grow: moving, sensing, and eating. Pick one to enjoy together today.
