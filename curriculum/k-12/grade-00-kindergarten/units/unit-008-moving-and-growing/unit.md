# Unit 8 — Moving & Growing

## Overview

This unit returns to the body, this time in motion and in growth. Building on *My
Body, My Home*, children practice big and small movements with growing control, sort
everyday and sometimes foods, and use their senses to notice and enjoy the world. Big
motor play is essential and abundant here, honoring every body and every pace of
growth as worthy.

## Essential questions

- How does my body grow stronger through movement and food?
- How do my senses help me notice and enjoy the world?
