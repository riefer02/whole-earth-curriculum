# Unit 1 — Who Am I?

## Overview

This first unit helps Kindergarten learners notice and name their feelings and begin
to build a positive sense of self. It is intentionally gentle: the goal is emotional
vocabulary and belonging, not performance.

## Essential questions

- What am I feeling right now, and how do I know?
- What makes me me, and what do I share with others?
