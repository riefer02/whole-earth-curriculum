# Lesson 7 — Things We Share

## Summary

Learners move from "what makes me unique" to "what we all share." They name the
needs, feelings, and experiences every person has in common — and see that sharing
connects them to everyone, near and far.

## Objectives

- Share one thing that makes them unique and one thing they share with others.
  _(D01.S2.K.01)_

## Connection

No matter where people live — in a busy city, a village, by the sea, or in the
mountains — everyone eats, sleeps, laughs, and cries. Everyone has feelings and needs
care. The things we share are like a bridge from me to you, and to people everywhere.

## Materials

- Paper and crayons
- A long strip of paper or a length of string

## Preparation

- Clear space for a "step in" circle.
- Prepare a list of shared things: eat, sleep, grow, laugh, cry, need water, need
  care, have feelings.

## Facilitator note

This lesson is written to the learner ("you") so it works in a classroom and for a
self-directed learner. As facilitator, keep the shared list universal — needs and
feelings every human shares — so every learner can step in and belong. This lesson
pairs with Lesson 6: uniqueness and sharedness are two halves of one truth.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Stand in a circle. Think: what do you and your best friend
   both do every day?
2. **Step in (15 min).** I will call out something. If it is true for you, step
   forward. "You eat food." "You sleep." "You laugh." "You cry." "You grow." "You
   have feelings." "You need people who care for you." Step in each time — look
   around: everyone stepped in!
3. **A bridge of sharing (10 min).** On the long paper, draw one thing everyone
   shares — a plate of food, a bed, a smiling face. Link your drawing to a partner's.
4. **Near and far (5 min).** Close your eyes and think of a child far away, in a
   place you have never seen. They eat and sleep and laugh too — just like you.
5. **Close (5 min).** Remember: I am me, and I share with everyone. Both are true.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Call out one shared thing at a time and let learners nod or point
  instead of stepping.
- **Extension:** Invite learners to name a shared thing not on the list and explain
  why everyone needs it.

## Assessment

- **Formative (observation):** Can the learner name one thing they share with all
  people?
- **Artifact (portfolio):** The shared "bridge" drawing.
