# Lesson 10 — All About Me

## Summary

The unit closes with a celebration. Learners gather their saved work — feelings,
body maps, "Me" sheets, and people drawings — into a small "Me" book and share who
they have discovered themselves to be.

## Objectives

- Share one thing that makes them unique and one thing they share with others.
  _(D01.S2.K.01)_
- Name the groups to which they belong (family, class, community). _(D01.S2.K.02)_

## Connection

You have spent many days learning about you — your feelings, your body, the things
that make you unique, and the people and groups you belong to. Now it is time to put
it all together and see the whole picture: who am I, all together?

## Materials

- All saved artwork from Lessons 2–9
- A folder or folded paper to make a "Me" book
- Crayons, glue, or string to bind the book

## Preparation

- Gather each learner's saved work from the unit.
- Fold or prepare a simple book cover for each learner.
- Arrange a sharing circle for the celebration.

## Facilitator note

This lesson is written to the learner ("you") so it works in a classroom and for a
self-directed learner. As facilitator, this is a celebration, not a test. Hold space
for each learner to share one page — some will share boldly, others quietly; both are
fine. The "Me" book becomes the portfolio artifact for the whole unit.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Spread out everything you have made in this unit. Look at it
   all — this is you, on paper.
2. **Build my "Me" book (20 min).** Put your pages in order: your feelings, your
   body map, what makes you me, the things we share, your people, your community. Add
   a cover with your name. Bind it with glue or string.
3. **Three big things (10 min).** Think of three things you learned about yourself:
   one feeling you know, one thing that makes you unique, and one group you belong
   to. Whisper them to yourself, then tell a partner.
4. **Celebrate (20 min).** In a circle, each learner shows one page and says one
   thing: "I am ___." The group answers: "You are you, and that is good."
5. **Close (5 min).** Hold your book to your heart. Remember: you know yourself
   better now than before.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Offer a sentence starter — "One thing I know about me is ___." — and
  allow showing a page without words.
- **Extension:** Invite learners to write or draw on the last page "something I am
  still learning about me."

## Assessment

- **Portfolio:** The completed "Me" book, reviewed with the learner.
- **Self:** The learner names one feeling, one unique thing, and one group they
  belong to.

## Home connection

Take your "Me" book home and share it with your people. Read it together and let
them add one thing they know about you.
