# Lesson 8 — Fairness & Caring for Others

## Summary

Learners gather everything from the unit into one four-part "kind choice" page: a
kind or helpful act, a fair idea, a group decision or class job, and a way to make
things right. They share their page and build a class "Fair & Caring" book.

## Objectives

- Name something kind or helpful to do for a friend or animal and say why it is
  good. _(D09.S1.K.01)_
- Tell when something is fair or unfair in a simple situation like sharing or taking
  turns. _(D09.S2.K.01)_
- Take part in a group decision or a small job that helps the class. _(D09.S3.K.01)_
- Use words (or a grown-up's help) to solve a small disagreement and make things
  right. _(D09.S4.K.01)_

## Connection

Fairness and caring are not one big thing — they are many small things you can do
every day: a kind act, a fair share, a helpful job, and a repair after a small hurt.
When you put them together, you are practicing how to live well with other people —
at home, in class, and anywhere people are together.

## Materials

- A four-box "kind choice" page per learner
- Crayons and drawing tools
- The class job chart
- Stapler, string, or clips for a class book

## Preparation

- Prepare a four-box page for each learner (or one large shared sheet): "kind &
  helpful," "fair," "our decision or job," "make it right."
- Set up a gathering place and a spot to assemble the class "Fair & Caring" book.

## Facilitator note

Written to the learner ("you"). This is the unit's capstone: each child authors their
own small statement of how to be fair and caring. Keep all four boxes optional in
depth — a word, a mark, or a drawing counts. This is a celebration of what learners
can *do*, not a test of what they know; there is no "correct" page. Celebrate each
page as a real contribution. The class job chart stays live after this lesson, so
the unit's habits continue beyond the page.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Remember the unit: kind and helpful acts, fair and unfair,
   deciding together and class jobs, and making things right. You have practiced all
   four.
2. **Fill the four boxes (20 min).** On your page, draw or write one thing in each
   box: something kind or helpful; something fair; one group decision or class job
   you took part in; and one way to make things right after a small hurt.
3. **Share (10 min).** With a partner or small group, show your page and tell about
   one box you are proud of. Listen to theirs.
4. **Make the class book (5 min).** Add your page to the "Fair & Caring" book or
   wall. Now everyone's kind choice is together.
5. **Close (5 min).** Fairness and caring are many small things you can do every
   day. Your choices matter — to your friends, your class, and you.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Complete one box at a time with a grown-up scribe; a single word or
  mark in each box counts.
- **Extension:** Invite learners to add a fifth box: "tomorrow" — one fair or caring
  thing they will do next.

## Assessment

- **Summative (portfolio):** The four-part "kind choice" page shows a kind act, a
  fair idea, a shared decision or job, and a repair.
- **Observation:** Does the learner connect each box to the right idea from the unit
  and share it in their own words?
