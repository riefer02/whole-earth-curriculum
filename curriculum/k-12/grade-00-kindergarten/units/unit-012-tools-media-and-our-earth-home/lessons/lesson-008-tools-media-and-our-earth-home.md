# Lesson 8 — Tools, Media & Our Earth Home

## Summary

This capstone brings the whole unit together. Learners build a four-part "Earth home"
picture: a tool that helps, a real or make-believe picture, a living thing and what it
needs, and one small act of care. They celebrate that tools, stories, and caring for
living things all belong to our one shared home.

## Objectives

- Name a tool or device people use and tell what it helps them do. _(D10.S1.K.01)_
- Tell the difference between a real thing they see and a made-up picture or story.
  _(D10.S2.K.01)_
- Observe and name a living thing and something it needs to live (sun, water, food).
  _(D10.S3.K.01)_
- Do one small act of care for the environment and say why it helps. _(D10.S4.K.01)_

## Connection

You have spent this unit learning four big things: tools help people, stories can be
real or pretend, living things need care, and small acts of care keep the Earth home
healthy. Today we put them all together, because they all live in the same home — the
Earth we share.

## Materials

- Large paper for a class "Earth home" picture (a big circle divided into four parts)
- Paper, crayons, glue, and found natural materials

## Preparation

- Draw or mark a big circle — the Earth — divided into four parts, labeled: Tool,
  Real/Pretend, Living, Care.
- Gather paper, crayons, glue, and found natural materials for adding to each part.

## Facilitator note

This lesson is written to the learner ("you"). As facilitator, this is a celebration,
not a test. Help each learner recall one thing from each part of the unit and add it to
the shared Earth home picture. Keep the tone joyful, hopeful, and inclusive — every
tool, every story, every living thing, and every act of care is welcome. The Earth is
our shared home, and we meet it with care, never with worship or fear.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Look at the big Earth circle. This is our shared home. Today we
   fill it with everything we learned.
2. **Part one — Tools (10 min).** Name one tool you learned about and the job it helps
   with. Draw or place a mark for it in the "Tool" part of the Earth.
3. **Part two — Real and pretend (10 min).** Say one real thing and one make-believe
   thing. Draw a mark in the "Real/Pretend" part to show you can tell them apart.
4. **Part three — Living (10 min).** Name one living thing and one thing it needs to
   live. Draw it in the "Living" part of the Earth.
5. **Part four — Care (10 min).** Do one small act of care right now, or name one you
   did this week, and say why it helps. Add a mark to the "Care" part.
6. **Celebrate (5 min).** Step back and look at the full Earth home. Tools, stories,
   living things, and care — all together, in one home, cared for by many hands.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Work on one part of the Earth at a time, or let the learner add just one
  mark to a shared class picture.
- **Extension:** Invite learners to make a small four-page "Earth home" book, one page
  for each part, and to add a page about a new way they want to help.

## Assessment

- **Portfolio:** The four-part "Earth home" picture or book becomes a portfolio
  artifact capturing the whole unit.
- **Observation (formative):** Can the learner recall one thing from each of the four
  strands — a tool, a real-or-pretend story, a living thing and its need, and an act
  of care?

## Home connection

Show your "Earth home" picture to a grown-up and tell them the four things you
learned: a tool and its job, a real thing and a pretend thing, a living thing and what
it needs, and one act of care you did. Ask them what small act of care they like to do
for the Earth.
