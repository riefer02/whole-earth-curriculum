# Unit 12 — Tools, Media & Our Earth Home

## Overview

This unit introduces children to the tools and media humans make, and to the living
Earth they depend on. They name a tool and what it helps do, tell the difference
between a real thing and a made-up picture or story, observe a living thing and what
it needs to live, and do one small act of care for the environment. Technology is met
with neither worship nor fear, and the Earth is met as a home to care for — not a
resource to consume.

## Essential questions

- How do tools help us, and how do we tell real from make-believe?
- How do we care for the living Earth that is our home?
