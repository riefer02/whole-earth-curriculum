# Lesson 1 — Then and Now

## Summary

Learners meet "then and now" as a gentle way to talk about the past. They sort
pictures and objects into "then" and "now" and name one thing that changed.

## Objectives

- Tell a "then and now" story about themselves or their family and how something
  changed. _(D08.S1.K.01)_

## Connection

Everything around you was once different: a tiny seed grows into a tall plant, and a
baby grows into a child who can run and jump. "Then" is how things were before; "now"
is how they are today. People everywhere tell then-and-now stories to remember their
past.

## Materials

- "Then" and "now" picture cards (pairs such as a baby and a child, a seed and a
  plant, a candle and a lamp)
- Paper and crayons

## Preparation

- Gather or draw pairs of "then" and "now" cards.
- Set up two labeled spots: a "then" spot and a "now" spot.

## Facilitator note

This lesson is written to the learner ("you") so it works in a classroom and for a
self-directed learner. Prepare the cards and the two labeled spots, then guide the
sorting and the closing story — the learner follows the procedure directly. Keep
"then" gentle and personal; there are no wrong pasts, and no one's way of living is
called better or worse.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Sit comfortably. Think of one thing that was different a long
   time ago — a smaller you, a shorter plant, a colder day.
2. **Meet "then" and "now" (10 min).** Look at the two spots. "Then" means before;
   "now" means today. Hold up a card pair: which is then, which is now? Say it aloud
   and point.
3. **Sort together (15 min).** Take turns placing cards on the "then" spot or the
   "now" spot. Say why you chose that spot: "This one is then because it is smaller."
4. **Tell your own story (10 min).** Choose one pair and tell a tiny then-and-now
   story about it: "Then it was a seed. Now it is a plant."
5. **Close (5 min).** Remember: every person, every place, and every thing has a
   "then" and a "now."

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Sort just two card pairs; model "then" with a clear gesture toward the
  "before" spot.
- **Extension:** Invite learners to draw their own then-and-now pair and explain it.

## Assessment

- **Formative (observation):** Can the learner place a card on the correct spot and
  say "then" or "now" — or point to the correct spot?
- **Formative (oral):** Does the learner tell one tiny then-and-now story?
