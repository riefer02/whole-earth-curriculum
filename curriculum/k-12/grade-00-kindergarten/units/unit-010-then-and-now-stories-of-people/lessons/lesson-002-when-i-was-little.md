# Lesson 2 — When I Was Little

## Summary

Learners tell their own "then and now" story by comparing "little me" and "now me."
They draw both and notice one thing that changed and one that stayed the same.

## Objectives

- Tell a "then and now" story about themselves or their family and how something
  changed. _(D08.S1.K.01)_

## Connection

You were once small enough to be carried, and now you can walk and run and tell
jokes. Growing up is a "then and now" story that is happening to you right now. The
parts that stay the same — the things you have always loved — are part of your story
too.

## Materials

- Paper and crayons
- A photo from home (optional; never required)

## Preparation

- Set out paper and crayons.
- Prepare a two-frame drawing sheet if using the enriched option.

## Facilitator note

Written to the learner ("you") so it works in a classroom and for a self-directed
learner. **Sensitivity is essential here:** never require a baby photo or a story of
early life. Some children were adopted, fostered, or separated from early caregivers;
"little me" can mean any earlier time they remember or imagine. Never press a child
who does not want to share. There is no "correct" family history.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Sit comfortably and think: what were you like when you were
   little?
2. **What changed? (10 min).** Take turns acting it out. Then you could maybe only
   crawl or babble; now you can run, jump, and sing. Show it with your body.
3. **Draw "little me" and "now me" (15 min).** Draw two pictures side by side. If you
   have no photo, draw from memory or from what a grown-up told you.
4. **Compare (10 min).** Point to one thing that changed (bigger feet, taller,
   stronger). Point to one thing that stayed the same (still love the same song, the
   same person, the same game).
5. **Close (5 min).** You have grown and changed — and some parts of you stay the
   same. Both are your story.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Draw only "now me" and tell one thing you used to do differently.
- **Extension:** Add a "someday me" frame and tell what you hope will be the same and
  different then.

## Assessment

- **Formative (observation):** Can the learner name one thing that changed and one
  that stayed the same?
- **Artifact (portfolio):** The two-frame "little me / now me" drawing.
