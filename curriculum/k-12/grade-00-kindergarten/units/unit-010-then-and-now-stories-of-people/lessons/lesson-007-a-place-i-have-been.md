# Lesson 7 — A Place I Have Been

## Summary

Learners talk about and draw a place they know — near or far, big or small — and
describe it with their senses. Every place a person has been is part of their story.

## Objectives

- Talk about a place they have been or a family story of moving, and draw or describe
  it. _(D08.S3.K.01)_

## Connection

You have been to places that are yours alone: a corner of your room, a market, a
park, a grandparent's home. Each place holds sights, sounds, and smells only you
remember. Sharing a place is sharing a little piece of your own map of the world.

## Materials

- Paper and crayons or paints

## Preparation

- Set out drawing materials.
- Prepare a few sense-word prompts: What did you see? hear? smell? feel?

## Facilitator note

Written to the learner ("you") so it works in a classroom and for a self-directed
learner. **No travel is required.** "A place" can be a room, a doorstep, a path, a
tree — any place a child knows. Some children have moved often or have few places
they call their own; let "here" and "now" count fully. Never rank places as more or
less special. There is no "correct" place.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Close your eyes and think of a place you know well. It can be
   big or small, near or far.
2. **Describe it (10 min).** Tell a partner or the group about it using your senses:
   what did you see, hear, smell, or feel there?
3. **Draw it (15 min).** Draw your place. Add one detail that only you would know —
   a crack in the step, the color of the door.
4. **Share (10 min).** Show your drawing and say one sentence about your place.
5. **Close (5 min).** Every person has places that matter. No two people's places are
   exactly alike, and each is worth hearing.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Draw the place together with a grown-up while describing it aloud.
- **Extension:** Add people to the drawing and tell who was there with you and what
  you did together.

## Assessment

- **Formative (observation):** Can the learner describe a place using at least one
  sense word (saw, heard, smelled, felt)?
- **Artifact (portfolio):** The place drawing with its spoken caption.
