# Unit 10 — Then & Now: Stories of People

## Overview

This unit introduces history as a gentle "then and now," told through stories and
artifacts rather than dates. Children tell a then-and-now story about themselves or
their family, listen to stories and songs from other cultures, talk about a place they
have been or a family story of moving, and retell gentle stories about fairness. The
past is held as a story of many peoples, never one nation's triumph.

## Essential questions

- How have people lived in the past, and how do we know?
- What stories do people from many places and times tell?
