# Lesson 8 — Brushing My Teeth

## Summary

Learners practice brushing their teeth step by step — morning and night, all surfaces,
for about two minutes — and discover that clean teeth help a body eat, talk, and smile.
They learn that people around the world keep teeth clean in different ways.

## Objectives

- Show a self-care routine (brushing teeth) with growing independence. _(D03.S4.K.01)_

## Connection

Your teeth work hard every day — biting, chewing, and helping you talk and smile. They
are a part of your body worth caring for. People everywhere clean their teeth, though
not all the same way: some use a brush and paste, some use a clean finger, and in many
places people have long used a small chewing stick. Caring for teeth is caring for
your body.

## Materials

- A toothbrush and toothpaste (each learner's own, or a demonstration brush)
- A cup of water for rinsing
- A large tooth model or picture

## Preparation

- Ensure each learner has their own clean toothbrush (never share brushes).
- Set up a place to practice, with water and a place to spit safely.
- Have a two-minute song ready to time the brushing.

## Facilitator note

This lesson is written to the learner ("you"). As facilitator, never share toothbrushes
and supervise so toothpaste is not swallowed. **Honor every context:** where brushes or
paste are unavailable, teach a clean-finger or chewing-stick method without shame.
Where a learner cannot brush their own teeth, a caregiver or older peer can help — the
goal is clean teeth, not doing it alone.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Smile at a friend. Your teeth help you eat, talk, and smile.
   Today you will learn to care for them.
2. **Meet the toothbrush (5 min).** Hold your brush. A little water and a small dab of
   toothpaste is all you need — a dot the size of a pea.
3. **Learn the steps (10 min).** Practice the motion with your finger first:
   - Brush the **tops** (the chewing part) with small circles.
   - Brush the **fronts**.
   - Brush the **backs**.
   - Brush the **tongue** side gently too.
   - Spit out the foam — do not swallow the paste.
4. **Brush for real (15 min).** Brush your own teeth, going around every part. Sing a
   short song in your head — about two minutes is enough. Rinse with a little water.
5. **When do we brush? (5 min).** Name the times: in the **morning** and before **bed**.
   Share with a partner one time you will remember.
6. **Close (5 min).** Feel your teeth with your tongue — smooth and clean. Clean teeth
   are a way of saying "I care for my body."

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Practice with a finger first, then brush together; use a picture card
  showing the steps.
- **Extension:** Invite learners to make up their own two-minute brushing song and to
  count how many teeth they can feel with their tongue.

## Assessment

- **Formative (observation):** Can the learner show the brushing steps and name one
  time to brush, with growing independence?
- **Self:** The learner feels their teeth and reports whether they feel clean.
