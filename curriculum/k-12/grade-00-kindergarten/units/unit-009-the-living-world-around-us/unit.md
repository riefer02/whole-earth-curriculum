# Unit 9 — The Living World Around Us

## Overview

This unit turns the child's wonder outward to the natural world. Building on the big
question of *Where Did Everything Come From?*, children now look closely at what lives
and changes around them — day and night, the seasons, and the growth of living things —
and practice asking "why" and "how" with their own ideas. Science is offered as
observation and wonder, never as a set of facts to memorize.

## Essential questions

- What lives around us, and how does the world change from day to night and season to
  season?
- How do we ask "why" and look closely to find answers?
