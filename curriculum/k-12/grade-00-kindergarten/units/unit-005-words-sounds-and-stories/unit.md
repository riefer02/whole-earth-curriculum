# Unit 5 — Words, Sounds & Stories

## Overview

This unit welcomes learners into the shared world of language. Children listen to
stories and retell what matters, begin to recognize the letters or characters of their
home writing system, draw and dictate their own ideas, and share greetings across
languages. The home language is honored first; multilingualism is treated as a wealth,
not a deficit, and every way of speaking is respected.

## Essential questions

- How do I listen, speak, and tell my own story?
- How do letters and marks carry meaning, and how do we honor many languages?
