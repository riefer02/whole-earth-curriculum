# Unit 4 — We Are Friends

## Overview

This unit lives out what *Who Am I?* began: now that learners can notice their own
feelings, they practice living well with others. Through turn-taking, sharing,
reading a friend's feelings, naming the communities they belong to, and helping care
for shared spaces, children learn that relationship is a set of skills to practice,
not rules to obey — in a spirit of mutual aid rather than competition.

## Essential questions

- How do we live well together — sharing, taking turns, and helping?
- What is a community, and how do we belong to one?
