# Lesson 13 — Caring for Our Classroom

## Summary

Learners put helping hands to work on the shared space itself. They wipe, sort, and
tidy the classroom together, and see that caring for a shared space is caring for the
people in it — and a small model of caring for our shared Earth.

## Objectives

- Care for a shared space when shown how. _(D02.S4.K.01)_

## Connection

Our classroom belongs to everyone, so everyone helps care for it. When you wipe a
table or put a toy back, you are not just cleaning — you are caring for every friend
who uses that space. The same is true of bigger shared spaces: a path, a well, a
garden, a river, the Earth itself.

## Materials

- Cleaning and tidying tools (cloth, small broom or brush, basket)
- Paper and crayons

## Preparation

- Choose simple care jobs learners can do: wipe a table, sort blocks, straighten
  books, water a plant.
- Gather the tools and place them where learners can reach them.

## Facilitator note

This lesson is written to the learner ("you") so it works in a classroom and for a
self-directed learner. As facilitator, show each job first ("watch, then try") and
keep it joyful — a tidy-up song or a shared goal beats a command. Frame care as
belonging: we care for what is ours together, not because we must obey but because we
love the space and the people in it.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Look around the room. It is our shared space — everyone's.
   Who should care for it? All of us, together.
2. **Learn the care jobs (10 min).** I will show you how: wipe the table, sort the
   blocks, straighten the books, water the plant. Watch, then choose one.
3. **Care for our space (15 min).** Do your care job. Work beside your friends. If
   you finish, look for another way to help.
4. **Notice the change (10 min).** Step back and look. How does the room feel now?
   We made it welcoming for everyone.
5. **Close (5 min).** Remember: caring for our shared space is caring for each other.
   What we do here, we can do for our wider world.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Pair with an older buddy and do one job together, step by step.
- **Extension:** Invite learners to plan one new care job for the class and explain
  why it matters.

## Assessment

- **Formative (observation):** Can the learner complete a care job for the shared
  space when shown how?
- **Artifact (portfolio):** A before-and-after drawing or note about the cared-for
  space.

## Home connection

At home, care for one shared space — sweep a step, tidy a corner, water a plant. Tell
a grown-up how the space looks and feels afterward.
