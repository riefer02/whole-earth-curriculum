# Lesson 10 — My Class Community

## Summary

Learners widen the circle from family to their class. They name their class as a
community they belong to and share one thing the class does together — and see that
every small part they play helps make the class a caring place.

## Objectives

- Name their class as a community they belong to and one thing people do together in
  it. _(D02.S3.K.01)_

## Connection

Your class is a community — a group of people who learn, play, and help each other
together, day after day. Everyone in it belongs, and everyone has a part. When each
person adds their small part, the whole class becomes a place that cares for everyone
in it.

## Materials

- Paper and crayons
- One large shared paper for a class "what we do together" picture

## Preparation

- Prepare the shared paper for a group drawing.
- Think through the class's daily routines to prompt memory.

## Facilitator note

This lesson is written to the learner ("you") so it works in a classroom and for a
self-directed learner. As facilitator, keep the focus on *togetherness* — not rules.
The class community is built by shared activities, not by obedience. Name every
learner as a member with something to contribute, so no one feels like a visitor.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Think: who is in our class? All of us. Together, we are a
   community.
2. **What we do together (10 min).** Name things the class does together: circle
   time, songs, games, cleaning up, sharing snacks, helping each other. Mime one and
   let others guess.
3. **Everyone has a part (15 min).** Each of us brings something to the class — a
   smile, a helping hand, a question, a song. Tell a partner one thing you add.
4. **Draw it (10 min).** On the shared paper, draw one thing our class does together.
   Add your mark. Look — many hands made one big picture, just like our class.
5. **Close (5 min).** Remember: your class is your community. You belong here, and
   the class is better because you are in it.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Offer a sentence starter: "In our class we ___ together."
- **Extension:** Invite learners to name one way the class could care for each other
  even better, and share it.

## Assessment

- **Formative (observation):** Can the learner name the class as a community and one
  thing the class does together?
- **Artifact (portfolio):** The shared "our class together" picture (or a photo of
  it).
