# Unit 13 — All of Me, All of Us

## Overview

This closing unit gathers the whole year together. Children describe feelings with
words, drawings, or movement; name the groups they belong to; name a community and
something people do together in it; and help care for a shared space. The final week
is a celebration of self and belonging — each child shares a portfolio of their own
work, and the class notices how everyone is both unique and connected.

## Essential questions

- Who have I become this year, and what do I know about my feelings and my
  communities?
- How do we celebrate all of me and all of us?
