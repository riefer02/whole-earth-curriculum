# Unit 12 — All of Me, All of Us

## Overview

The year closes by gathering all of it together. Learners revisit and celebrate their
feelings, the story of who they are, the communities they belong to, and the care
they have practiced all year. Through a portfolio celebration — sharing an "All
About Me" page, writing and number work, made things, and a remembered kind act —
each child sees themselves as a whole person and sees their class as a caring
community to which they belong. This unit spirals back to the year's opening and
sends the learner forward into Grade 2.

## Essential questions

- Who have I become this year, and who are we together?
- How do I celebrate myself and my community?
