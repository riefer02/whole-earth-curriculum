# Unit 10 — Fairness & Our Shared Rules

## Overview

This unit begins the discipline of ethics as a living practice. Learners name a kind
and an unkind choice and give a reason why a story character made a good or hurtful
choice; describe a fair and an unfair way to share and name one thing everyone in
the class needs to feel safe; help make a class rule or choice with a reason and do a
small job that helps the classroom or school; and use words to say how they feel in a
conflict, listen to a friend, and suggest a way to make things right. Questions are
held as questions, not answered as dogma — fairness is practiced, not preached.

## Essential questions

- What is fair, and how do we decide together?
- How do we solve problems and make things right?
