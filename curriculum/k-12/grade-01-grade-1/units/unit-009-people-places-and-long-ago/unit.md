# Unit 9 — People, Places & Long Ago

## Overview

This unit steps into the wider human story, gently and personally. Learners sequence
three or four events from their own day or week and compare one thing from long ago
with one thing today; describe a family or community tradition and compare how two
families or communities celebrate or mark an event; describe the place where they
live and use a simple map or globe to find it and one other place; and tell when a
story character is treated unfairly, and one kind act where someone helped another.
History is "then and now," told from many perspectives, never a single nation's
triumph.

## Essential questions

- How do I tell the story of time — what came before, now, and next?
- How do people live in different places, and what can I learn from them?
