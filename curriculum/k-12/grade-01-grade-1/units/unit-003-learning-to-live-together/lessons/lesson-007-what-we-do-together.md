# Lesson 7 — What We Do Together

## Summary

Learners choose one community they belong to, describe one thing the group does
together, and name one way they help out. They discover that a community is kept
strong by many small acts of care, not by one big hero.

## Objectives

- Describe one thing each community does together and one way they help out.
  _(D02.S3.01.02)_

## Connection

Communities do things together: a family shares a meal, a class learns, a
neighborhood keeps a place clean or celebrates a festival. And in every community,
each person helps in their own small way. All those small helps are what hold a
community together.

## Materials

- A "Together / help out" chart
- Crayons and paper

## Preparation

- Draw a two-column chart: "What we do together" and "How I help out."
- Arrange a space for drawing and sharing.

## Facilitator note

Keep "help out" concrete and small — wiping a table, watering a plant, carrying
groceries, feeding a pet — so every learner can see their own place in it. Honor
that some families do paid work and some do care work at home; both are real help.
Connect care for shared spaces to caring for the Earth where it arises. Read the
procedure aloud; help learners fill in the chart.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Sit in a circle. Think: what is one thing your family or class
   does all together?
2. **Together and help out (10 min).** Look at the chart. "Together" is what the
   group does (eat, learn, clean, celebrate). "Help out" is what one person does to
   make it happen (set the table, water the plant, sweep). Name one of each for one
   community.
3. **Choose and share (15 min).** Pick one community you belong to. Tell a partner:
   one thing we do together, and one way I help out.
4. **Draw it (10 min).** Draw your community doing its thing together, with you in the
   picture helping out. Show yourself helping out in the picture.
5. **Close (5 min).** Remember: no one does everything; everyone does something. Small
   helps make a strong community.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Provide sentence frames: "My ___ does ___ together. I help by ___."
- **Extension:** Ask learners to name a second community and compare how the two help
  out differently.

## Assessment

- **Formative (observation):** Can the learner name one "together" and one "help out"
  for a community?
- **Artifact (portfolio):** The "together and helping" drawing, kept in the learner's
  portfolio.
