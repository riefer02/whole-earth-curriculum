# Unit 3 — Learning to Live Together

## Overview

This unit lives out what *My Feelings, My Story* began: now that learners can name
their own feelings, they practice living well with others. They use kind words,
listen without interrupting, read a classmate's feelings from face, voice, and body,
and guess what another person might need. They name the communities they belong to
and how each one works together, and they practice care — offering help without
being asked and looking after shared materials. Relationship is taught as a set of
skills to practice, in a spirit of mutual aid rather than competition.

## Essential questions

- How do we speak, listen, and help one another well?
- What communities do I belong to, and how do I help care for them?
