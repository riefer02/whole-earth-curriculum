# Unit 7 — Making, Music & Movement

## Overview

This unit gives the whole body and the imagination to making. Learners create a
drawing or painting with lines, shapes, and colors and look at an artwork, noticing
something and a feeling it gives them; sing a simple song and keep a steady beat,
telling loud from soft and fast from slow; move their body to show a feeling and
pretend to be a character using voice, face, and body; and build something with a
simple plan, then test it and change one thing to make it work better. Art is a way
of knowing and sharing the world — every culture's arts are living heritage, not a
museum piece.

## Essential questions

- How do I make and share what I see, hear, and feel?
- How can I plan, make, and improve something of my own?
