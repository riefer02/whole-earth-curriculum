# Unit 5 — Numbers All Around Us

## Overview

This unit begins formal numeracy. Learners count, read, and write whole numbers to
120 and use them to say how many; add and subtract within 20 using objects, drawings,
and counting strategies; identify, extend, and create repeating patterns and find a
missing number in a simple number sentence; compose and take apart two- and
three-dimensional shapes; measure and compare with non-standard units; and collect,
organize, and answer questions about simple data, telling time and using coins in
real situations. Mathematics is taught as sense-making with real things — counting,
pattern, and shape drawn from many traditions, for everyone.

## Essential questions

- How do numbers help me count, compare, and solve problems in the world?
- Where do I find patterns and shapes in the things around me?
