# Unit 8 — Healthy, Safe & Strong

## Overview

Returning to the body with growing independence, this unit turns from senses and
movement to health and safety. Learners name a variety of foods from different groups
and understand that food gives the body energy to grow, play, and learn; perform
daily self-care routines with growing independence; and learn that their body
belongs to them, and to tell a trusted adult if someone makes them feel unsafe or
uncomfortable. Health is taught without shame, honoring the wide range of ways
families eat and live.

## Essential questions

- What keeps my body healthy and strong?
- How do I keep myself safe and ask for help when I need it?
