# Lesson 10 — Our Earth Home: Tools, Stories, and Care

## Summary

This capstone brings the whole unit together. Learners build a four-part "Earth home"
picture: a tool and how it makes work easier, a real photo and a drawing with its maker
and audience, a living and a nonliving thing and a shared need, and a waste-less act of
care for a living thing. They celebrate that tools, stories, living things, and care all
belong to one shared home.

## Objectives

- Name a tool people use at home or at school and tell what job it helps them do.
  _(D10.S1.01.01)_
- Describe one way a tool makes a task easier or faster than doing it by hand.
  _(D10.S1.01.02)_
- Tell the difference between a real photo and a drawing or a pretend story, and give
  one reason. _(D10.S2.01.01)_
- Say who made a simple picture, poster, or story and who it was made for.
  _(D10.S2.01.02)_
- Tell the difference between a living thing and a nonliving thing, and give an example
  of each. _(D10.S3.01.01)_
- Name two things a plant and an animal both need to live, such as water, food, light,
  or air. _(D10.S3.01.02)_
- Name one way to waste less, such as turning off water or reusing a bag, and do it.
  _(D10.S4.01.01)_
- Help care for a plant or animal and say what it needs each day. _(D10.S4.01.02)_

## Connection

You have spent this unit learning four big things: tools help people, and they make
work easier or faster; pictures and stories are made by someone for someone, and we can
tell a real photo from a drawing or a pretend story; some things are living and some
are not, and plants and animals both need water and air; and we can waste less and care
for living things. Today we put them all together, because they all live in the same
home — the Earth we share.

## Materials

- Large paper for a class "Earth home" picture (a big circle divided into four parts)
- Paper, crayons, glue, and found natural materials

## Preparation

- Draw or mark a big circle — the Earth — divided into four parts, labeled: Tools,
  Stories, Living, Care.
- Gather paper, crayons, glue, and found natural materials for adding to each part.

## Facilitator note

This lesson is written to the learner ("you"). This is a celebration, not a test. Help
each learner recall one thing from each of the four strands and add it to the shared
Earth home picture. Keep the tone joyful, hopeful, and inclusive — every tool, every
story, every living thing, and every act of care is welcome, and none is ranked. Hold
the unit's whole stance gently: technology met with neither worship nor fear, stories
met with wonder not suspicion, the Earth met as home not storehouse, and learners met
as helpers not saviors. For a learner who is blind or has low vision, they may add a
found object by feel to each part while a partner describes the part; a non-speaking
learner adds marks and points while a partner names them. Every body is honored, and
every body's contribution counts.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** Look at the big Earth circle. This is our shared home. Today we
   fill it with everything we learned.
2. **Part one — Tools (10 min).** Name one tool and the job it helps with, and say one
   way it makes work easier or faster. Draw or place a mark for it in the "Tools" part.
3. **Part two — Stories (10 min).** Name one real photo and one drawing or pretend
   story, and say who made one of them and who it was for. Draw a mark in the
   "Stories" part to show you can tell them apart and name a maker.
4. **Part three — Living (10 min).** Name one living thing and one nonliving thing,
   and say two things a plant and an animal both need (water and air). Draw them in
   the "Living" part.
5. **Part four — Care (10 min).** Do one small waste-less act of care right now — turn
   off a tap, reuse a bag, water a plant with just enough — and say what a living
   thing needs each day. Add a mark to the "Care" part.
6. **Celebrate (5 min).** Step back and look at the full Earth home. Tools, stories,
   living things, and care — all together, in one home, cared for by many hands.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Work on one part of the Earth at a time, or let the learner add just one
  mark to a shared class picture with a partner's help.
- **Motor differences:** Direct a partner's hands to add each mark while the learner
  says what it stands for; the choosing and the saying are the contribution.
- **Blind/low vision:** Add found objects by feel to each part while a partner
  describes the part and names what the learner chose.
- **Deaf/hard of hearing:** Share each part with sign, pictures, and acting; the
  learner signs or points to the mark they add and to the part it belongs to.
- **Extension:** Make a small four-page "Earth home" book, one page for each part, and
  add a fifth page about a new way the learner wants to help.

## Assessment

- **Portfolio:** The four-part "Earth home" picture or book becomes a portfolio
  artifact capturing the whole unit.
- **Observation (formative):** Can the learner recall one thing from each of the four
  strands — a tool and how it helps, a photo-vs-drawing and its maker, a living and
  nonliving thing and a shared need, and a waste-less act of care?

## Home connection

Show your "Earth home" picture to a grown-up and tell them the four things you
learned: a tool and its job, a real photo and a pretend story and who made them, a
living and nonliving thing and a shared need, and one waste-less act of care you did.
Ask them what small act of care they like to do for the Earth.
