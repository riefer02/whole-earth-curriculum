# Unit 11 — Tools, Stories & Our Earth Home

## Overview

This unit meets technology, media, and the living Earth with neither worship nor
fear. Learners name a tool people use at home or school and describe how it makes a
task easier or faster; tell a real photo from a drawing or pretend story and say who
made a picture, poster, or story and for whom; tell a living thing from a nonliving
thing and name two things a plant and an animal both need; and practice small acts of
care — wasting less and helping care for a plant or animal. Ecology is not a chapter;
it is the ground we stand on.

## Essential questions

- What tools do we use, and how do they help us?
- How do I tell what is real and true, and how do I care for the Earth?
