# Unit 4 — Letters, Sounds & Words

## Overview

This unit begins formal literacy. Learners listen and ask or answer simple
questions, speak in complete sentences, decode simple words by sounding out letters
and letter groups in their home writing system, and read a simple text aloud,
retelling its beginning, middle, and end. They write letters and simple words and
compose a sentence or more with a capital letter and an end mark. Throughout, the
home language is honored first and multilingualism is treated as wealth: learners
share their own languages with pride and learn a greeting and a courtesy word in
another. Foundational skills are taught with explicit instruction and guided
practice, per [`docs/philosophy.md`](../../../../docs/philosophy.md) §13.

## Essential questions

- How do letters and sounds come together to make words I can read and write?
- How do I share my ideas with words, in my own language and others'?
