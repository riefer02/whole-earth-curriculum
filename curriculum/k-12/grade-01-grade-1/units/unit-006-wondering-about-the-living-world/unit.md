# Unit 6 — Wondering About the Living World

## Overview

This unit opens the year's science as inquiry. Learners use the five senses to
observe and describe objects, plants, animals, and weather, and ask a question about
something they notice, offering a guess they can check. They observe what living
things need and sort things into living and nonliving; describe the properties of
materials and how objects move (push, pull, roll, slide); and notice the patterns of
the Sun, Moon, and stars, day and night, and the weather and seasons where they live
and elsewhere. Big questions stay open — science is wondering, observing, and
weighing evidence, never dogma.

## Essential questions

- What is alive, and what do living things need?
- How can I ask questions and look closely to find things out?
