# Grade 4 Scope

## How to read this scope

Each `unit_id` in the frontmatter resolves to a unit file under `units/`.
`start_day`/`end_day` map the unit onto the 180-day school year; the list is a
continuous sequence from day 1 to day 180 with no gaps or overlaps. `domain` and
`pillar` name the primary domain(s) and pillar(s) each unit serves. `domain_weighting`
expresses the intended balance of instructional time across the ten domains, in
percent of the year. `lesson_count` counts the core lessons; the days between lessons
carry flex time, assessment, integration, and celebration. `assessment_plan`
describes how a child's growth is seen and recorded at this level.

## Year at a glance

The Grade 4 year is a spiral that deepens what Grade 3 began, and it opens inward.
*Feelings in Full Color & My Growing Self* (U.04.001, D01) builds on Grade 3's changing
feelings: children now describe the strength of a feeling, recognize complex feelings
such as jealousy and pride, explain how their experiences shape them, and describe a
family tradition that connects them to a community. *My Body at Work & at Rest*
(U.04.002, D03) reads the body with new precision — how heartbeat and breathing change
with activity and rest, and the many ways bodies sense and move, including people who
use assistive tools.

*Listening, Perspective & Belonging* (U.04.003, D02) turns outward into the practice of
care. Where Grade 3 learned to take turns and speak without blame, Grade 4 learns to
listen actively — paraphrasing before responding — and negotiate fairly. The heart of
the year belongs to the two great tools of school, now working with real independence.
*Reading, Writing & Many Languages* (U.04.004, D04) grows comprehension and craft:
summarizing main ideas, using context clues and word parts, carrying a piece of writing
through plan, draft, revise, edit, and publish — a peer's language honored,
multilingualism held as wealth. *Numbers, Patterns, Shapes & Data* (U.04.005, D05)
reaches place value to 1,000,000, multi-digit operations checked by estimation, area and
perimeter by formula, and line plots with fractions — mathematics as sense-making with
real things, for everyone.

From there the year looks out at the world. *Controlled Experiments & the Living World*
(U.04.006, D06) turns wonder into a careful, repeated test: measuring with tools,
comparing results across trials, and following how living things in an ecosystem depend
on one another, and how energy moves as heat, sound, and light. *Making, Music &
Movement* (U.04.007, D07) gives craft to the imagination — notation read and played, a
dance form from another culture met as a living tradition, and design for a specific
user — while *Healthy, Safe & Strong* (U.04.008, D03) returns to the body with growing
independence: reading food labels, honoring the many ways families eat well, safety
rules, and respectful behavior online.

The final third steps into the wider human story, told in many voices. *People, Places &
Long Ago* (U.04.009, D08) places events from two places on a dated timeline, compares
daily life then and now, compares how two cultures explain the same natural event
through story and observation, describes a knowledge tradition such as star navigation
or oral history, and shows on maps how landforms and water shaped settlement. *Rights,
Fairness & Our Shared Rules* (U.04.010, D09) weighs two reasons for a choice, explains
dignity and where it is honored or denied, and asks whether a rule is fair to all it
affects. *Tools, Media & Our Earth Home* (U.04.011, D10) compares two tools for the same
job, checks a claim against a second source, and asks how one action helps or harms a
place. The year closes in *All of Me, All of Us* (U.04.012, D01 + D02), celebrating each
whole person and the caring community, and looking ahead to Grade 5.

Throughout, the four pillars are interwoven. Intellectual awareness (P3) carries the
most weight — reading toward chapter books and non-fiction, multi-digit number work, and
repeated experiments are the year's central work — but emotional and social awareness
(P1) and physical and somatic awareness (P2) remain strong, for a known, cared-for, and
capable body-and-self is the ground all formal learning stands on. Contextual and
ecological awareness (P4) keeps growing: history toward dated timelines and knowledge
traditions, civics toward rights, dignity, repairing harm, and peaceful resistance, and
ecology toward ecosystems and stewardship. No high-stakes tests anywhere: growth is seen
through reading aloud, writing, number work with real things, and a portfolio of what
they make, say, and do.
