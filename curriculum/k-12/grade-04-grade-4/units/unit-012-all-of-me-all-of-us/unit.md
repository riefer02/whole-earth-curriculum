# Unit 12 — All of Me, All of Us

## Overview

This unit gathers the year together, building on Grade 3's closing celebration.
Learners reflect on how their interests, abilities, and experiences shape their sense
of self, describe a family or cultural tradition and how it connects them to a larger
community, and plan the steps of a goal for the year ahead. They identify a role they
can play in their class or neighborhood community and carry it out, describe how
communities welcome newcomers and why belonging matters, and organize a small act of
care — celebrating each whole person, the caring community they have built, and the
road toward Grade 5.

## Essential questions

- Who am I, and who am I becoming?
- How do I belong, care, and act for the common good?
