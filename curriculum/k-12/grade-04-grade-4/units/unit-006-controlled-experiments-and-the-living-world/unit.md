# Unit 6 — Controlled Experiments & the Living World

## Overview

This unit turns wonder into a careful, repeated test, building on Grade 3's fair test
of changing one thing. Learners use simple tools such as a ruler, scale, thermometer,
or magnifier to measure and record observations with units, and compare results across
repeated trials, explaining why repeating a test matters. They describe how animals'
body parts and behaviors help them survive in their environment, how living and
nonliving things in an ecosystem depend on one another, how energy moves from one
place or object to another as heat, sound, and light and is never destroyed, build a
simple circuit and explain what conductors and insulators do, describe the water cycle
and how water is reused around the Earth, and explain how fossils and rock layers give
clues about Earth's long history. Big questions are explored as stories grounded in
evidence, never as dogma.

## Essential questions

- How do careful measurement and repeated trials make an experiment trustworthy?
- How do living and nonliving things, energy, and the Earth's long story connect?
