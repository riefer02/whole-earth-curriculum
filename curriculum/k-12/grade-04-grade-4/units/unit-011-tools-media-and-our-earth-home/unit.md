# Unit 11 — Tools, Media & Our Earth Home

## Overview

This unit meets the made world and the living Earth, building on Grade 3's simple
machines, ads-versus-information, and food chains. Learners explain what it means to
design a tool and describe one step in designing, compare two tools for the same job
and say which works better and why for a given person or situation, identify the
author, purpose, and audience of a message and explain how they shape it, and check
whether a simple claim is true by finding a second source and comparing them. They
describe an ecosystem as living and nonliving parts that depend on each other, explain
the jobs of producers, consumers, and decomposers, explain what happens to trash after
it is thrown away and name "reduce, reuse, recycle" in order of what is best to do
first, and investigate how one human action — such as littering or planting — can help
or harm a local place. Technology and media are met with critical judgment, neither
worship nor fear.

## Essential questions

- How are tools designed, and how do I read a message's purpose and audience?
- How do living systems work, and how can I care for my place on Earth?
