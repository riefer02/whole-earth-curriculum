# Unit 1 — Feelings in Full Color & My Growing Self

## Overview

This unit opens the year inward, building on Grade 3's work of describing how feelings
change and mix. Learners now describe the *strength* of a feeling as a little, some,
or a lot and what each level feels like, recognize complex feelings such as jealousy,
guilt, and pride and connect them to real situations, explain how their interests,
abilities, and experiences shape their sense of self, and describe a family or
cultural tradition and how it connects them to a larger community. They plan the
several steps of a goal, try the plan, and adjust when a step does not work, and
practice self-control by staying with a task or rule even when it is hard or tempting
to quit. Every family's shape and every child's pace are honored.

## Essential questions

- How strong is a feeling, and how do I know?
- What makes me, me — and what is my next step?
