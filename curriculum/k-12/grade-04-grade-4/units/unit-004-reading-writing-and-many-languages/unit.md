# Unit 4 — Reading, Writing & Many Languages

## Overview

This unit is the heart of the year's literacy work, building on Grade 3's fluency and
paragraph writing. Learners summarize the main idea and key details of an oral
presentation or read-aloud, present a short talk to a group with clear volume and
pace, use context clues and word parts such as roots, prefixes, and suffixes to figure
out unfamiliar words, and compare and contrast themes, characters, or ideas within and
across two texts. They write a narrative, informative, or opinion piece organized into
paragraphs and carry it through a simple writing process — plan, draft, revise, edit,
and publish — on their own. They learn that languages belong to families and that many
languages share roots, and show respect for a peer's language by learning and
correctly pronouncing their name and a few words. Home language is honored first, and
multilingualism is treated as wealth.

## Essential questions

- How do I understand and share the ideas I read and hear?
- How do I carry an idea through the whole writing process, and how are languages related to one another?
