# Unit 8 — Healthy, Safe & Strong

## Overview

This unit returns to the body with growing independence, building on Grade 3's sense
of what a body needs and where food comes from. Learners read simple nutrition
information on food packages and use it to make a healthier choice, and describe how
different families and cultures eat different healthy foods, treating every way of
eating with respect. They name and practice basic safety rules for home, road, water,
and weather — fire, crossing, swimming, and storms — and explain safe and respectful
behavior online, including not sharing private information and where to get help. The
body is taught with dignity and joy, never shame, and the wide range of bodies,
families, and ways of eating is honored.

## Essential questions

- How do I use food information to make choices that keep me well?
- How do I keep myself safe at home, near roads and water, in weather, and online?
