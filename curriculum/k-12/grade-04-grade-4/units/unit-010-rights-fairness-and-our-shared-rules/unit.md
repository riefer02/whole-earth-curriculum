# Unit 10 — Rights, Fairness & Our Shared Rules

## Overview

This unit deepens ethics and civic life, building on Grade 3's weighing of reasons and
repairing harm. Learners weigh two reasons for a choice and decide which is stronger
and why, and describe what a value is and name one of their own values. They explain
what dignity means and give an example of it being honored or denied, evaluate whether
a rule or practice is fair to everyone it affects, describe how people make decisions
and choose leaders in their community and compare with one other place in the world,
work with classmates on a small shared project dividing tasks fairly and listening to
each person, practice active listening and "I" statements to solve a disagreement
fairly, and describe restorative practice — listening, taking responsibility, and
repairing harm — and why it can work better than punishment alone. Questions are held
as questions, not answered as dogma.

## Essential questions

- How do I weigh reasons, values, and what is fair?
- How do people make decisions together, and how do we repair harm?
