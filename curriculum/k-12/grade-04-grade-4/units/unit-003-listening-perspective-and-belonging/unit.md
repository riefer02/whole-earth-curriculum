# Unit 3 — Listening, Perspective & Belonging

## Overview

This unit turns outward into the practice of care, building on Grade 3's turn-taking
and "I" statements. Learners now practice active listening by paraphrasing what a
partner said before responding, and negotiate a fair solution to a disagreement with a
peer with minimal adult help. They explain why a person might feel the way they do
using clues about what happened, compare their own point of view with a classmate's on
the same event, identify a role they can play in their class or neighborhood community
and carry it out, describe how communities welcome newcomers and why belonging
matters, organize a small act of care, and explain the difference between sharing for
the common good and taking more than one's share.

## Essential questions

- How do I really hear someone, and how do we solve a disagreement fairly?
- Why do people see the same thing differently, and how do communities make everyone feel they belong?
