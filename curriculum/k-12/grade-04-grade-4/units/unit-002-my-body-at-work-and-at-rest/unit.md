# Unit 2 — My Body at Work & at Rest

## Overview

This unit reads the body with new precision, building on Grade 3's sense of how two
senses work together. Learners describe how the body changes with activity and rest —
heartbeat, breathing, and warmth — and why, and notice and respect differences in how
bodies sense and move, including people who use aids or assistive tools. They learn
and follow the rules of simple cooperative games and sports while playing fairly, and
apply movement skills such as balance, agility, and coordination to a variety of
activities, including adapted or seated versions. The wide range of human bodies and
abilities is honored with dignity and joy.

## Essential questions

- How does my body change when I move and when I rest, and why?
- How do we play fairly and welcome every kind of body?
