# Unit 7 — Making, Music & Movement

## Overview

This unit gives craft to the imagination, building on Grade 3's pattern, rhythm, dance,
and design. Learners create an artwork that shows depth or space by overlapping, size,
or placement, and identify the subject, materials, and likely purpose of an artwork
from another culture, naming one respectful question about it. They read and play or
sing a simple melody or rhythm from basic notation, describe how the music of another
culture uses its own instruments, scales, or rhythms, learn and perform a simple dance
or movement form from another culture and describe its meaning or occasion, create a
character using voice, body, and a simple prop, design and make something for a
specific user and need with feedback to improve it, and explain how an object from
another culture reflects that culture's materials, needs, and ideas. The arts of many
cultures are honored as living heritage, not museum pieces.

## Essential questions

- How do artists show depth, feeling, and meaning?
- How do the arts of many cultures teach us to see with respect and curiosity?
