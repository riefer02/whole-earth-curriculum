# Unit 5 — Numbers, Patterns, Shapes & Data

## Overview

This unit grows number work from Grade 3's place value to 1,000. Learners use place
value to 1,000,000 to read, write, round, and compare multi-digit whole numbers, and
add, subtract, multiply, and divide multi-digit whole numbers, using estimation to
check that answers are reasonable. They generate and analyze a number or shape pattern
that follows a given rule, use a letter or symbol to stand for an unknown number in a
simple equation, draw and identify points, lines, line segments, rays, and angles and
classify shapes by their lines and angles, apply formulas for the area and perimeter
of rectangles, convert measurements within a system to solve problems involving
distance, time, volume, and money, and make and interpret line plots with fractional
measurements. Mathematics is taught as sense-making with real things, drawn from many
traditions, for everyone.

## Essential questions

- How do I work with very large numbers and check that my answers make sense?
- How do patterns, shapes, measurement, and data help me make sense of the world?
