# Unit 14 — All of Me, All of Us

## Overview

This unit gathers the year together, building on Grade 8's closing celebration.
Learners return to their coherent personal narrative, connecting past, present, and
hoped-for future, and reflect on how their society shapes them and how they in turn
shape it back. They set the longer-term goal with a realistic plan and milestones
that will carry them into Grade 10, compare forms of community across cultures, and
design a cooperative project that meets a real need in their community. They
celebrate each whole person, the caring community they have built, and the living
Earth — and look ahead to Grade 10, where a personal narrative becomes a considered
position, identity is examined as socially and historically constructed, and the
ethics of technology and the environment deepen into full political and ecological
stewardship.

## Essential questions

- What coherent story now connects my past, present, and hoped-for future, and how does my society shape me while I shape it back?
- How do I set the longer-term goal that will carry me into Grade 10, and how do I stand with others in the communities I care about?
