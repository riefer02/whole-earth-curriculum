# Unit 13 — Technology, Media & Our Earth Home

## Overview

This unit gathers the outward year into critical judgment and stewardship — neither
worship nor fear — building on Grade 8's framed images, deepfakes, and ecosystem
resilience toward *algorithmic and ecological systems thinking*. Learners explain how
software and algorithms, such as recommendations and feeds, shape what people see and
choose, and analyze who controls a digital platform and what power that control gives
them over users. They explain how algorithmic feeds and echo chambers can narrow what
people see and how to break out of them, and evaluate a viral claim by tracing it to
its source and checking it against independent reporting. They explain the greenhouse
effect and how the carbon cycle connects living things, oceans, atmosphere, and rock
over time, explain what a feedback loop is and how warming can speed up more warming,
compare fossil fuels and renewable energy sources in how they work, their limits, and
their effects on people and the planet, and analyze how the burdens and benefits of
environmental change fall unevenly on different people — environmental justice.

## Essential questions

- How do algorithms and platforms shape what I see and choose, and who controls them and what power does that control give them?
- How do echo chambers narrow what I see and how do I break out of them, and how do I trace a viral claim to its source?
- How does the carbon cycle connect living things, oceans, atmosphere, and rock, and how do feedback loops, energy choices, and environmental justice shape our future?
