# Unit 3 — Feelings, Identity & Purpose

## Overview

This unit opens the year inward, building on Grade 8's separation of event, thought,
and feeling and its analysis of multiple, intersecting identities. Learners now
*consolidate* identity, values, and purpose: they use a broad emotional vocabulary to
articulate subtle and secondary emotions in complex social situations, and analyze how
emotions are shaped by social context, media, and group belonging. They construct a
coherent personal narrative connecting past, present, and hoped-for future, analyze
how a person's identity is shaped by society and in turn shapes their choices, set a
longer-term goal with a realistic plan and milestones, and advocate for their own
needs and boundaries while considering others. Every self is honored as a whole in the
making, never a label, and every question is held as a question.

## Essential questions

- How do I name the subtle and secondary emotions of complex situations, and how are my emotions shaped by social context, media, and belonging?
- What coherent story links my past, present, and hoped-for future, and how does my society shape who I am while I also shape it?
- How do I set a longer-term goal with a realistic plan, and advocate for my own needs while honoring others?
