# Lesson 1 — Feelings, Identity & Purpose: Where We've Been, Where We're Going

## Summary

Learners **recall four Grade-8 ideas about the self** — separating event, thought, and feeling;
intersecting identities; inner worth versus outside approval; and short- versus long-term
consequences — and **preview this unit's three strands and six goals**. They meet the unit's
organizing idea: a whole self is built from **feelings**, **identity**, and **purpose**, and it is
always in the making.

## Objectives

- Begin to name the unit's first goal: using a broad emotional vocabulary to articulate subtle and
  secondary emotions. _(D01.S1.09.01)_
- Begin to name the unit's fourth goal: constructing a coherent personal narrative linking past,
  present, and hoped-for future. _(D01.S2.09.01)_
- Begin to name the unit's fifth goal: setting a longer-term goal with a realistic plan and
  milestones. _(D01.S3.09.01)_

## Connection

Think of a moment recently when you felt something you could not quite name — a mix that was not
simply happy or sad, but something sharper and smaller, like a prick of envy at a friend's good news
followed by a flush of guilt at feeling envious at all. Think of the story you tell about who you
are when someone asks, and of the things you are hoping to become. Feelings, identity, and purpose
are not three separate subjects; they are the one life you are already living. This unit helps you
see it clearly and, a little more, steer it.

## Materials

- Unit map sheet
- Recall cards

## Preparation

- Copy the unit map sheet and recall cards.
- Retrieval: from Grade 8, recall (a) separating the event, the thought, and the feeling; (b)
  intersecting identities (culture, gender, ability, language); (c) inner worth versus outside
  approval; and (d) short-term versus long-term consequences. Today we link those to the new unit.

## Facilitator note

This lesson is written to the learner ("you"). It is the unit's **opening and retrieval pass**, so
the goals are modest: **(1) recall four Grade-8 ideas — a feeling can be pulled apart into event,
thought, and feeling (S-332); a self is made of intersecting identities (D01.S2.08.01); self-esteem
can come from inner worth or from outside approval (S-222); and choices have short- and long-term
consequences (D01.S3.08.02); (2) preview the unit's three strands — emotional literacy, identity and
self-concept, self-regulation and agency — and its six goals; (3) plant the organizing idea: a whole
self is built from *feelings*, *identity*, and *purpose*, and it is always in the making.** Do not try
to teach the six goals today; just name and locate them. The **social-emotional** lens: naming the
self precisely is a skill that supports regulation and care (S-001). The **global** lens: every
culture gives its young people ways of answering "who am I?", and the answers differ by place and
time. The **ethics** lens: the distinction between how things *are* and how things *ought* to be runs
through the whole unit — a feeling is a fact about you, while what you do with it is a choice
(philosophy §5). Keep every question open: identity is held as a story in the making, never a label
or a verdict (philosophy §11). Spacing note (S-039): this recall pass is what makes the unit's ideas
stick later.

## Procedure

1. **Recall (10 min).** In a small group, look at four recall cards: **event / thought / feeling**,
   **intersecting identities**, **inner worth vs. outside approval**, **short- vs. long-term
   consequences**. For each, say in one sentence what you remember from Grade 8.
2. **Connect the four (5 min).** What do all four have in common? (Each is about *knowing yourself*
   — noticing what is going on inside you and choosing what to do with it.)
3. **Meet the map (10 min).** Look at the unit map. Three strands: **emotional literacy**,
   **identity & self-concept**, **self-regulation & agency**. Six goals — two per strand. Read each
   goal aloud once; don't worry about understanding them yet.
4. **Plant the big idea (10 min).** The unit's idea in one breath: a whole self is built from
   **feelings** (what moves inside me), **identity** (the story of who I am), and **purpose** (what I
   choose to become). Say it back in your own words.
5. **Wonder (10 min).** Write one question about each strand in the wonder box. Which question do
   you most want answered by the end of the unit?
6. **Close (5 min).** Today we remembered what we know and saw the road ahead. Next: a richer feeling
   vocabulary.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Give sentence starters for the recall round ("A feeling can be pulled apart into…").
- **Extension:** Add a fifth Grade-8 idea of your own and say why it will matter in this unit.

## Assessment

- **Formative (observation):** Can the learner recall each Grade-8 idea in one sentence and name the
  unit's three strands and six goals?
- **Artifact:** The completed unit map (or the learner's written recall), kept in the portfolio.

## Home connection

Ask someone at home: what is one thing you once believed about yourself, then changed your mind
about as you grew? Bring the story (no names needed) to the next lesson.
