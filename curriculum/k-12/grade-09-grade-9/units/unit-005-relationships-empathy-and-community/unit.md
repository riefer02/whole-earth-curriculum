# Unit 5 — Relationships, Empathy & Community

## Overview

This unit turns outward into the practice of care in a plural world, building on
Grade 8's facilitation of conflict and its analysis of power and belonging. Learners
communicate respectfully across differences of opinion, culture, and background,
practice healthy relationship skills including consent, trust, and honest talk about
feelings, and use empathic inquiry to understand another's feelings and needs before
responding. They examine how empathy can be limited or expanded by distance, media,
and technology, compare forms of community across cultures including chosen families
and mutual-aid networks, analyze how nationalism, exclusion, and in-group loyalty can
both build and limit community, evaluate how economic and social systems shape
cooperation, competition, and inequality, and design a cooperative project that meets
a real need in their community.

## Essential questions

- How do I communicate across real difference and practice healthy relationships built on consent, trust, and honest talk about feelings?
- How do I understand another's feelings and needs before responding, and how is empathy limited or expanded by distance, media, and technology?
- How are communities built and limited across cultures, and how do systems shape cooperation, competition, and inequality?
