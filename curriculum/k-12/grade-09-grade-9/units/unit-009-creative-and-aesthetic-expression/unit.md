# Unit 9 — Creative & Aesthetic Expression

## Overview

This unit gives craft and a critical eye to the imagination, building on Grade 8's
series of artworks, compositions, devised pieces, and prototypes toward work made
*with and about* technology. Learners create a digital or mixed-media artwork that
combines techniques and reflect on the choices technology made possible, and analyze
how visual media such as advertising, news, and social media use composition and
symbolism to influence viewers. They create music using digital or recording tools
and reflect on how the technology shaped the sound and the process, and analyze how
music can build identity, community, or resistance across more than one culture. They
create a performance that uses the moving body to explore identity or a personal
question, analyze how dance, drama, and ritual build community or carry memory across
cultures, use digital or fabrication tools to design and make an object and reflect
on how the tools changed what was possible, and analyze how technology and design
shape everyday life and the environment. The arts of other cultures are met as living
traditions to honor with respectful questions, never as specimens.

## Essential questions

- How do I make digital and mixed-media art, and how do advertising, news, and social media use composition and symbolism to influence viewers?
- How do I create music with digital tools, and how can music build identity, community, or resistance across cultures?
- How do I use the moving body and the maker's hand to explore identity and to shape everyday life and the environment?
