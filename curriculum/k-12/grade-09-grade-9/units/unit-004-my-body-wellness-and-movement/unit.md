# Unit 4 — My Body, Wellness & Movement

## Overview

This unit reads the body as the somatic ground of thought and feeling, building on
Grade 8's reading of the nervous system and stress. Learners explain the somatic basis
of emotion and thought, showing how body state shapes mood and thinking, and evaluate
body-image messages from media and peers to articulate a body-positive or
body-neutral view of their own body. They set a personal movement or fitness goal and
track progress over time, analyze how movement affects mental health and learning,
analyze a local food system from farm to table to waste and its effects on health,
workers, and the environment, and explain how hunger, food security, and unequal
access to healthy food shape health outcomes around the world. The body is taught
with dignity and joy, honoring the wide range of bodies and abilities.

## Essential questions

- How does my body state shape my mood and thinking, and how do I hold a body-positive or body-neutral view against media's messages?
- How do I set and track a movement goal, and how does movement shape my mental health and learning?
- How does the food system work from farm to table to waste, and who does it nourish, harm, and leave out?
