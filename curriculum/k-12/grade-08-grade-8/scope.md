# Grade 8 Scope

## How to read this scope

Each `unit_id` in the frontmatter resolves to a unit file under `units/`.
`start_day`/`end_day` map the unit onto the 180-day school year; the list is a
continuous sequence from day 1 to day 180 with no gaps or overlaps. `domain` and
`pillar` name the primary domain(s) and pillar(s) each unit serves. `domain_weighting`
expresses the intended balance of instructional time across the ten domains, in
percent of the year. `lesson_count` counts the core lessons; the days between lessons
carry flex time, assessment, integration, and celebration. `assessment_plan`
describes how a learner's growth is seen and recorded at this level.

## Year at a glance

The Grade 8 year deepens what Grade 7 began. Where Grade 7 named contradiction, Grade 8
dissects: pulling a reaction apart, weighing competing accounts, modeling with a line,
and critiquing a work with evidence rather than taste. This is the top of the
middle-school band: rapid, uneven growth is named as normal, never as shame.

The year opens inward. *Feelings, Identity & Purpose* (U.08.001, D01) separates a strong
reaction into event, thought, and feeling, explains how emotion can inform and distort
judgment, holds identity as multiple and intersecting, and tells inner worth from
outside approval. *My Body, Movement & Wellness* (U.08.002, D03) reads the nervous
system's fight, flight, or rest response, names what stress does over time, designs a
routine for strength, endurance, and flexibility, and keeps play fair for every ability.
*Relationships, Empathy & Conflict* (U.08.003, D02) turns care into facilitation:
reading tone, body language, and digital messages, managing conflict toward a shared
solution, and telling charity from mutual aid.

The heart of the year belongs to argument and number, wielded critically. *Reading,
Writing, Argument & Many Languages* (U.08.004, D04) analyzes rhetoric — how word choice,
tone, and delivery persuade — moves into structured debate, reads texts from many
cultures and times, writes in multiple forms with sources cited, and examines how
language can include or exclude, home language first, with multilingualism held as
wealth. *Functions, Linear Equations, Geometry & Data* (U.08.005, D05) brings rational
and irrational numbers, functions and linear equations, transformations, the Pythagorean
theorem, and scatter plots modeled without mistaking association for cause. Mathematics
stays sense-making with real things, drawn from many traditions, for everyone.

From there the year looks out through systems, and the difference between a model and
the thing itself. *Science: Systems, Evidence & Models* (U.08.006, D06) designs
experiments and revises models when evidence changes, and studies genes and ecosystems,
the atom and the fields, and the stars and the moving crust — origins offered as
evidence-grounded models among many, never as creed. *Making, Composing, Devising &
Designing* (U.08.007, D07) builds a body of work around one theme and critiques art,
music, performance, and design with evidence about elements, context, and footprint.
*Healthy, Safe & Strong: Substance & Self-Care* (U.08.008, D03) plans a balanced meal,
explains how substances affect the body and brain, and builds a self-care routine.

The final third steps into the wider human story. *Trade, Knowledge Systems & the Human
Story* (U.08.009, D08) weighs competing explanations, compares knowledge traditions,
follows the Silk Roads and Indian Ocean trade routes, and analyzes slavery as a system
built on the denial of human dignity and how enslaved people resisted — history told
from many perspectives, never one people's triumph. *Ethics, Justice & Civic Power*
(U.08.010, D09) weighs consequences, duties, and fairness in a real dilemma, compares
ideas of justice, and weighs restorative responses to conflict. *Media, Technology & Our
Earth Home* (U.08.011, D10) weighs a technology's benefits and harms, reads altered
images and video, and applies sustainability to a community's care for land and water.
The year closes in *All of Me, All of Us* (U.08.012, D01 + D02), gathering every whole
person and the living Earth, looking ahead to high school.

Throughout, the four pillars are interwoven. Intellectual awareness (P3) carries the
most weight — argument and rhetoric, function and data reasoning, and systems thinking —
but emotional and social awareness (P1) grows markedly, and a known, cared-for
body-and-self remains the ground all learning stands on (P2). Contextual and ecological
awareness (P4) keeps rising: history toward competing explanations and knowledge
traditions, civics toward real dilemmas, and media and ecology toward altered images and
sustainability. No high-stakes tests anywhere: growth is seen through argument and cited
reading, function and data work, controlled investigations, and learners' portfolios.
Difficulty is named honestly and met with concrete agency and hope.
