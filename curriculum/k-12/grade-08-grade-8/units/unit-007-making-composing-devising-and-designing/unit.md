# Unit 7 — Making, Composing, Devising & Designing

## Overview

This unit gives craft to the imagination at the moment formal reasoning emerges fully,
building on Grade 7's making, performing, and designing with feedback. Learners create
a series of artworks exploring one theme or idea, reflecting on how the work changes
across the series, and critique an artwork using evidence about its elements, context,
and purpose, distinguishing observation from judgment. They compose a piece in a chosen
style or tradition and reflect on the choices that shaped it, and critique a musical
work using evidence about its elements, context, and effect. They choreograph or devise
a piece exploring one theme and reflect on how the work changed through rehearsal, and
critique a performance using evidence about its movement, expression, context, and
purpose. They design and make a working prototype that solves a defined problem and
revise it through testing and feedback, and critique a design using evidence about its
function, materials, fairness, and environmental footprint. The arts of other cultures
are met as living traditions to honor with respectful questions, never as specimens.

## Essential questions

- How do I create a series of artworks on one theme and critique art, music, and performance with evidence rather than mere taste?
- How do I compose, devise, and make a working prototype — and revise each through feedback and testing?
