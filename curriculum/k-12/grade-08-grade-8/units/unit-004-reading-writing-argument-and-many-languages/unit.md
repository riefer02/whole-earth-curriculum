# Unit 4 — Reading, Writing, Argument & Many Languages

## Overview

This unit is the heart of the year's argument work, building on Grade 7's evaluation of
a speaker's reasoning and credibility of sources toward the analysis of *rhetoric*.
Learners analyze how a speaker's word choice, tone, and delivery shape meaning and
persuade an audience, and engage in a structured debate or deliberation, arguing a
position with reasons while listening to opposing views. They analyze how point of
view, tone, and figurative language shape a reader's understanding of a text, and read
texts from multiple cultures and time periods, comparing how each reflects its context.
They write in multiple forms — narrative, argument, and informational text — with
coherent organization and voice, and cite sources accurately in writing, distinguishing
their own words and ideas from those of others. They analyze how language can include
or exclude people, and use digital translation tools critically — always in the
learner's home language first, with multilingualism held as wealth.

## Essential questions

- How do a speaker's word choice, tone, and delivery shape meaning and persuade, and how do I argue a position while truly listening to opposing views?
- How do point of view, tone, and figurative language shape what I understand, and how do texts from many cultures reflect their contexts?
- How do I write in many forms with my own voice, cite sources honestly, and notice how language can include or exclude?
