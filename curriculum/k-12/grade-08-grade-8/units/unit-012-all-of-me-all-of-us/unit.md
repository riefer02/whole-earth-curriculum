# Unit 12 — All of Me, All of Us

## Overview

This unit gathers the year together, building on Grade 7's closing celebration.
Learners reflect on how multiple and intersecting identities — culture, gender,
ability, and language — shape the whole self they are becoming, and name how they will
manage impulses deliberately, weighing short-term against long-term consequences as
they look ahead. They take part in strengthening belonging in their school or local
community, analyze how systems of care and mutual aid operate and what makes them fair
or unfair, and distinguish charity from mutual aid and solidarity, choosing to stand
with others rather than only to give from above. They celebrate each whole person, the
caring community they have built, and the living Earth — and look ahead to Grade 9,
where a coherent personal narrative, the ethics of technology and the environment, and
reasoning across multiple ethical frameworks will carry the same questions into high
school.

## Essential questions

- Who am I becoming, as multiple and intersecting identities shape a whole self, and what will I choose rather than react to as I look ahead?
- How do I strengthen belonging, understand systems of care, and act with solidarity rather than charity as I carry this year into high school?
