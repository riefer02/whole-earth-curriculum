# Unit 8 — Healthy, Safe & Strong: Substance & Self-Care

## Overview

This unit returns to the body at a turning point, building on Grade 7's media and body
image, food-marketing literacy, and first aid. Learners plan and prepare a simple
balanced meal using safe food-handling practices, and explain how eating patterns, not
only what one eats, affect health, digestion, and wellbeing. They explain how
substances such as tobacco, alcohol, and drugs affect the body and brain and how to
make safe, informed choices, and build a personal self-care routine covering sleep,
hygiene, movement, and connection, and track how it affects wellbeing. Every body,
every pace, and every family's way of eating is honored; the body is taught with
dignity, never shame.

## Essential questions

- How do I plan and prepare a simple balanced meal safely, and how do eating patterns — not only what I eat — shape my health and wellbeing?
- How do substances like tobacco, alcohol, and drugs affect the body and brain, and how do I build and track a self-care routine that keeps me well?
