# Unit 2 — My Body, Movement & Wellness

## Overview

This unit reads the body with new inward precision, building on Grade 7's naming of
internal body signals and its reading of how movement changes during puberty. Learners
explain the basic role of the nervous system in sensing and responding — the fight,
flight, or rest response — and describe how stress affects the body over time, naming
healthy ways to help the body return to calm. They design a short movement routine and
explain how each part builds strength, endurance, or flexibility, and demonstrate fair
play and inclusive practices that welcome peers of all abilities into movement and
games. The body is taught with dignity and joy, honoring the wide range of bodies and
abilities.

## Essential questions

- How does my nervous system sense and respond — fight, flight, or rest — and how does stress affect my body over time?
- How do I design movement that builds strength, endurance, and flexibility, and make games and movement welcome every ability?
