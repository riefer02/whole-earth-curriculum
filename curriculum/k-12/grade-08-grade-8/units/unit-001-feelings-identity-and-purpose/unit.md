# Unit 1 — Feelings, Identity & Purpose

## Overview

This unit opens the year inward, building on Grade 7's precise naming of mixed and
conflicting feelings and its reading of cultural norms around emotion. Learners now
*analyze*: they separate a strong emotional reaction into the event, the thought, and
the feeling, and explain how emotion can both inform and distort judgment, using a
personal example. They explain how multiple and intersecting identities — culture,
gender, ability, and language — shape a whole self, and analyze where self-esteem comes
from, distinguishing inner worth from outside approval. They manage impulses
deliberately, choosing a response rather than reacting in emotionally charged moments,
and explain the difference between short-term and long-term consequences and weigh them
in a decision. Identity formation now runs at full strength, so every self is honored
as a whole made of many intersecting parts, never a single label.

## Essential questions

- How do I separate the event, the thought, and the feeling in a strong emotional reaction, and how can emotion both inform and distort my judgment?
- Who am I as multiple and intersecting identities shape a whole self, and where does my sense of worth truly come from?
- What does it mean to choose my response rather than react, and to weigh short-term against long-term consequences?
