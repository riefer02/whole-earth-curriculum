# Unit 11 — Media, Technology & Our Earth Home

## Overview

This unit closes the outward year by gathering everything together, building on Grade
7's trustworthy sources, misinformation-versus-disinformation, and climate change.
Learners analyze the benefits and harms of a technology for different groups of people
and weigh them, and explain how design decisions involve trade-offs among cost, safety,
access, and the environment, using a real example. They analyze how a photo, video, or
story can be framed or edited to change its meaning and explain how to check it, and
evaluate whether an image or video might be altered or misleading, such as a deepfake,
and describe one way to check. They explain how an ecosystem responds to a disturbance,
such as fire or flooding, and what helps it recover — resilience — and compare two
biomes or climate zones and explain how the living things there are adapted to their
conditions. They explain what sustainability means — meeting today's needs without
robbing the future — and apply it to one resource, and analyze a community's practice
for caring for land or water, from any culture, and what makes it work.

## Essential questions

- How do I weigh a technology's benefits and harms for different people, and what trade-offs do design decisions carry?
- How can a photo, video, or story be framed, edited, or altered to mislead, and how do I check it?
- How do ecosystems recover from disturbance, how are living things adapted to their biomes, and what does it mean to live sustainably?
