# Unit 7 — Making, Performing & Designing

## Overview

This unit gives craft to the imagination at the moment abstract reasoning emerges,
building on Grade 6's full design cycle and its comparing of arts across cultures.
Learners create an artwork that communicates a personal message and revise it after
feedback, and explain how perspective, framing, or emphasis shapes what a viewer
notices and feels about an image. They perform a piece with attention to dynamics,
phrasing, and ensemble and revise after listening to a recording, explain how sound is
used in media — film, games, ads — to shape mood and meaning, perform a scene or dance
with attention to timing, expression, and ensemble and revise after feedback, and
explain how gesture, posture, and spacing communicate meaning without words. They
design something with accessibility in mind so more kinds of people can use it and
test it for barriers, and explain how a design choice trades off cost, materials,
function, and environmental impact. The arts of other cultures are met as living
traditions to honor with respectful questions, never as specimens.

## Essential questions

- How do I create an artwork with a personal message and revise it after feedback?
- How do dynamics, sound, gesture, and spacing shape what an audience notices and feels?
- How do I design for everyone, and weigh cost, materials, function, and environmental impact?
