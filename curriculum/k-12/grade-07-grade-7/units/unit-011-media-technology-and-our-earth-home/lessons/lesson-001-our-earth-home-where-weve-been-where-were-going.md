# Lesson 1 — Our Earth Home: Where We've Been, Where We're Going

## Summary

Learners open the unit by **retrieving** what they learned in Grade 6 and **previewing** the
four strands of "Media, Technology & Our Earth Home." They meet the unit map, write three
wonders, and sort them into the strands — starting the unit portfolio they will add to every
lesson.

## Objectives

- Preview the unit's eight learning goals — tracing technology spread, intended vs. unintended
  effects, source trustworthiness, mis/disinformation, matter and energy cycles, symbiosis,
  climate science, and individual vs. collective action. _(D10.S1.07.01, D10.S1.07.02, D10.S2.07.01, D10.S2.07.02, D10.S3.07.01, D10.S3.07.02, D10.S4.07.01, D10.S4.07.02 — preview)_

## Connection

Every human being on the planet shares one home — the same air, the same water, the same
stretches of forest and ocean. Into that home, people have made **technologies** (tools, phones,
machines) and **media** (messages, pictures, stories) that travel faster than ever before. Today
you begin a journey through four big questions about how those tools and messages work, how they
change the living Earth, and how we care for it — alone and together.

## Materials

- Unit map card
- Wonder slips

## Preparation

- Copy the unit map card and wonder slips.
- Retrieval: from Grade 6, recall four ideas — **who gets to use a technology** (D10.S1.06.02),
  **persuasive technique** (D10.S2.06.02), **biodiversity** (D10.S3.06.02), and **footprint**
  (D10.S4.06.01). Each returns this unit, at a deeper level.

## Facilitator note

This lesson is written to the learner ("you"). The ideas to land: **(1) this unit has four
strands — Technology & Society, Media & Information, Ecology & Earth Systems, and Stewardship &
Action — and they all meet in one shared "Earth home"; (2) we are building on Grade 6, not
starting over; (3) wondering is the engine — learners begin by asking, not by being told.**
Retrieval practice here is deliberate (spaced recall of last year's ideas — see Dunlosky et al.
2013, S-039); do not skip it. The ethics lens: stewardship is a question of what is right toward
people now and in the future. The egalitarian lens: ask early "who is included and who is left
out" — it will return every lesson. The global lens: the Earth home is one place seen from many
peoples and places; keep the frame broad, never one region's default. The technology &
environment lens: these two strands are the unit's heart — tools change the living Earth, and the
living Earth is what all tools depend on. Hold every wonder as valid; the goal is the asking, not
the answer.

## Procedure

1. **Gather and recall (12 min).** Think back to Grade 6. With a partner, recall: who got to use
   a technology and who did not? What was a persuasive technique? What did biodiversity mean?
   What was a footprint? Share one with the group.
2. **Meet the unit map (8 min).** Look at the unit map. The unit has four strands:
   **Technology & Society**, **Media & Information**, **Ecology & Earth Systems**, and
   **Stewardship & Action** — all around one **Earth home**. Notice the strands are not separate
   boxes; they are four ways of looking at one place.
3. **Write your wonders (15 min).** On three slips, write three things you wonder about tools,
   messages, the living Earth, or caring for it. There are no wrong wonders.
4. **Sort the wonders (10 min).** Place each slip under the strand it belongs to. If it fits two
   strands, put it between them — that is where the best questions live.
5. **Close (5 min).** Keep your wonders. At the end of the unit you will return to them and see
   what you now can say.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Give sentence starters ("I wonder what happens when ___", "I wonder who gets to
  ___").
- **Extension:** Write a "wonder + guess" pair — your question and your current best answer — and
  mark it to check again at the capstone.

## Assessment

- **Formative (observation):** Can the learner name the four strands and sort a wonder into one
  (or between two) with a reason?
- **Portfolio artifact:** The three wonder slips, kept as the first page of the unit portfolio.

## Home connection

Ask someone at home: "What is one thing people have made that changed how your family lives, and
one thing about the Earth you want to protect?" Bring their answer to mind as the unit unfolds.
