# Unit 11 — Media, Technology & Our Earth Home

## Overview

This unit closes the outward year by gathering everything together, building on Grade
6's work on who gets to use a technology, persuasive technique, biodiversity, and
footprints. Learners trace how one technology spread across the world and changed the
societies that adopted it, using a non-Western example where possible, and explain the
difference between an intended and an unintended effect of a technology. They explain
what makes a source trustworthy and compare two sources on the same topic for
reliability, and explain the difference between misinformation — false by mistake — and
disinformation — false on purpose — with an example of each. They explain how matter
and energy move through an ecosystem, such as the carbon in a forest, and are reused
rather than created new, describe a symbiotic relationship such as pollination and how
both organisms benefit, explain the basic science of climate change — greenhouse gases
trapping heat — and the main human activities that add to it, and compare individual
and collective actions for caring for the environment, explaining why both matter.

## Essential questions

- How does a technology spread and change societies, with intended and unintended effects?
- How do I tell a trustworthy source from an untrustworthy one, and misinformation from disinformation?
- How does matter and energy move through an ecosystem, and how do we care for a warming world — alone and together?
