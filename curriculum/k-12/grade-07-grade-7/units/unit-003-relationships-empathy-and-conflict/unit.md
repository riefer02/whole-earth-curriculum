# Unit 3 — Relationships, Empathy & Conflict

## Overview

This unit turns outward into the practice of care at the moment peers matter most,
building on Grade 6's work of navigating changing friendships and choosing assertion.
Learners set and respect personal boundaries and recognize when a peer has set one, and
repair a relationship after a disagreement by apologizing, listening, and agreeing on
next steps. They explain how a person's background, culture, and experiences shape
their point of view, and practice empathy across difference by seeking to understand
views unlike their own. They examine how online and offline communities differ and how
people belong in each, explain how communities change over time and why change can be
both hard and good, organize or join a mutual-aid action in their school or community,
and reflect on the motives for helping — care, duty, or recognition.

## Essential questions

- How do I set and respect boundaries, and repair a relationship after a disagreement?
- How does another person's background shape their point of view, and how do I practice empathy across real difference?
- How do online and offline communities differ, and how do I act for care and the common good?
