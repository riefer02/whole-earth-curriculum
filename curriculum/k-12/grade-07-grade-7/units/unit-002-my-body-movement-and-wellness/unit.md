# Unit 2 — My Body, Movement & Wellness

## Overview

This unit reads the body with new inward precision, building on Grade 6's mind–body
connection and its work of naming how emotions and body sensations connect. Learners
notice and name internal body signals — hunger, fatigue, stress — accurately in
themselves, and apply strategies such as spacing, timing, and teamwork in a game or
sport. They describe how movement changes during puberty and adapt activities to their
own changing body, and describe how energy needs differ among bodies and activities,
and why restricting food can harm a growing body. The body is taught with dignity and
joy, honoring the wide range of bodies and abilities, and the uneven, rapid growth of
these years is named as normal, never shameful.

## Essential questions

- How do I notice and name my body's internal signals — hunger, fatigue, stress — accurately?
- How do I move with skill and teamwork, and adapt movement to my own changing, growing body?
- How do energy needs differ among bodies, and why does fueling a growing body matter?
