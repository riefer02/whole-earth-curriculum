# Unit 12 — All of Me, All of Us

## Overview

This unit gathers the year together, building on Grade 6's closing celebration.
Learners reflect on how their identity has changed over time and predict the ways it
may keep growing, reflect on a past decision and its consequences to make a better
choice next time, and name the routine — study, sleep, movement — they will carry
forward to support their goals. They explain how communities change over time and why
change can be both hard and good, organize or join a mutual-aid action in their school
or community, and reflect on the motives for helping — care, duty, or recognition.
They celebrate each whole person, the caring community they have built, and the living
Earth — and look ahead to Grade 8, where multiple and intersecting identities, the
separation of event from thought from feeling, and deliberate management of impulse
will carry the same questions further.

## Essential questions

- Who am I becoming, with an identity that keeps changing, and what routine and reflection will I carry forward?
- How do I belong, care, and act for the common good — and carry it into Grade 8?
