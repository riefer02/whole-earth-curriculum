# Unit 1 — Feelings, Identity & Purpose

## Overview

This unit opens the year inward, building on Grade 6's work of identifying patterns in
one's own emotional responses and reading the feeling behind a behavior. Learners now
hold contradiction at once: they describe mixed or conflicting feelings — happy and sad
at once — in precise language, and explain how cultural norms shape which feelings are
shown, hidden, or valued. They reflect on how their identity has changed over time and
predict the ways it may keep growing, and recognize stereotypes and labels and explain
how these can limit a person's sense of self. They reflect on a past decision and its
consequences to make a better choice next time, and build and follow a personal routine
— study, sleep, movement — that supports their own goals. Identity formation now runs
in earnest, so every self is honored as a changing work in progress, never a verdict.

## Essential questions

- How do I hold mixed and conflicting feelings at once, and how do cultural norms shape what I show, hide, or value?
- Who am I as my identity changes over time, and how do stereotypes and labels try to narrow it?
- What does it mean to author my own life — to learn from a past decision and build a routine that serves my goals?
