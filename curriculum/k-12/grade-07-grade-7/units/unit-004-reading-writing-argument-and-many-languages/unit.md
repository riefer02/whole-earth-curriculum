# Unit 4 — Reading, Writing, Argument & Many Languages

## Overview

This unit gives the heart of the year to argument and number, building on Grade 6's
claim-with-counterargument writing and its citing of text evidence. Learners now
*evaluate*: they evaluate a speaker's reasoning, identifying unsupported claims and
asking probing questions, and deliver a presentation adapted to an audience and
purpose, using evidence to support their points. They analyze how an author develops
characters, setting, and plot — or central ideas — across a text, and evaluate the
credibility of a source by checking its author, purpose, and evidence. They develop
ideas with relevant, well-chosen details and precise language across a range of genres,
and give and use peer feedback to improve a piece of writing, explaining the choices
they made. They explain what translation and interpretation are and when each is
needed in everyday life, and recognize dialect and accent as natural variation,
challenging the idea that one way of speaking is correct — always in the learner's home
language first, with multilingualism held as wealth.

## Essential questions

- How do I evaluate a speaker's reasoning and build a presentation on evidence?
- How do I read how an author develops character, setting, and idea — and judge whether a source is credible?
- How do I write with precise language, use peer feedback, and honor every language and way of speaking?
