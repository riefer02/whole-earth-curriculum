# Grade 7 Scope

## How to read this scope

Each `unit_id` in the frontmatter resolves to a unit file under `units/`.
`start_day`/`end_day` map the unit onto the 180-day school year; the list is a
continuous sequence from day 1 to day 180 with no gaps or overlaps. `domain` and
`pillar` name the primary domain(s) and pillar(s) each unit serves. `domain_weighting`
expresses the intended balance of instructional time across the ten domains, in
percent of the year. `lesson_count` counts the core lessons; the days between lessons
carry flex time, assessment, integration, and celebration. `assessment_plan`
describes how a learner's growth is seen and recorded at this level.

## Year at a glance

The Grade 7 year deepens what Grade 6 began, and it is the year nuance arrives — when
the world stops being made of one thing at a time. Where Grade 6 found patterns in
feeling and saw the self as made of many parts, Grade 7 holds contradiction and hears
the reasons behind a rule. Abstract reasoning grows but still arrives unevenly in these
years, named as normal, never as shame. Every claim is met with a concrete anchor.

The year opens inward. *Feelings, Identity & Purpose* (U.07.001, D01) teaches learners
to name mixed feelings precisely, to see how cultural norms shape what may be shown, and
to recognize the stereotypes that would narrow an identity. *My Body, Movement &
Wellness* (U.07.002, D03) reads internal signals — hunger, fatigue, stress — adapts
movement to a changing body, and refuses the idea that any body is a problem to be
fixed. *Relationships, Empathy & Conflict* (U.07.003, D02) practices boundaries
respected, repair after disagreement, empathy across difference, and why we help.

The heart of the year belongs to the two great tools of school, now working critically.
*Reading, Writing, Argument & Many Languages* (U.07.004, D04) moves from making a claim
to evaluating one: weighing a speaker's reasoning, judging a source by its author,
purpose, and evidence, and revising after peer feedback — with dialect and accent met as
natural variation, never error, and home language first. *Proportions, Rational Numbers,
Equations & Chance* (U.07.005, D05) carries algebra further: proportional relationships,
scale, percentages, rational-number operations, two-step equations and inequalities,
sampling and bias, and probability. Mathematics stays sense-making with real things,
drawn from many traditions, for everyone.

From there the year looks out at the world through systems, weighing what a pattern
shows against what it proves. *Science: Systems, Evidence & Deep Time* (U.07.006, D06)
tells correlation from causation, weighs what evidence supports a claim, follows
variation and natural selection, and reads deep time in fossils and ice cores — origins
offered as models with evidence, never as creed. *Making, Performing & Designing*
(U.07.007, D07) gives craft to the imagination: creating and revising, performing with
attention to ensemble, and designing with accessibility in mind. *Healthy, Safe &
Strong: Puberty & Self-Care* (U.07.008, D03) returns to the body at a turning point —
media and body image, food marketing read critically, first aid, and boundaries for
social media.

The final third steps into the wider human story with the historian's and ethicist's
tools. *Civilizations, Knowledge Systems & the Human Story* (U.07.009, D08) weighs the
many causes of one change, honors non-European achievement, and sets indigenous
ecological knowledge beside formal science — colonization and forced migration told from
many perspectives, never one people's triumph. *Ethics, Justice & Shared Power*
(U.07.010, D09) asks who is helped, who is harmed, and what is fair; examines human
rights honored and violated; and practices restorative steps over punishment alone.
*Media, Technology & Our Earth Home* (U.07.011, D10) traces how a technology spread and
changed its adopters, tells misinformation from disinformation, follows the carbon
through a forest, and sets individual beside collective action. The year closes in *All
of Me, All of Us* (U.07.012, D01 + D02), gathering every whole person and the living
Earth, looking ahead to Grade 8.

Throughout, the four pillars are interwoven. Intellectual awareness (P3) carries the
most weight — argument and source evaluation, proportional and rational-number
reasoning, and systems-and-evidence thinking in science are the year's central work —
but emotional and social awareness (P1) grows markedly, and a known, cared-for
body-and-self remains the ground all formal learning stands on (P2). Contextual and
ecological awareness (P4) keeps rising: history toward multiple causes and knowledge
systems; civics toward real dilemmas and human rights; and media and ecology toward
trustworthy sources, misinformation, and climate change. No high-stakes tests anywhere:
growth is seen through argument and cited reading, number work with real things, and
learners' portfolios.
