# Grade 10 Scope

## How to read this scope

Each `unit_id` in the frontmatter resolves to a unit file under `units/`.
`start_day`/`end_day` map the unit onto the 180-day school year; the list is a
continuous sequence from day 1 to day 180 with no gaps or overlaps. `domain` and
`pillar` name the primary domain(s) and pillar(s) each unit serves. `domain_weighting`
expresses the intended balance of instructional time across the ten domains, in
percent of the year. `lesson_count` counts the core lessons; the days between lessons
carry flex time, assessment, integration, and celebration. `assessment_plan`
describes how a learner's growth is seen and recorded at this level.

## Year at a glance

The Grade 10 year carries Grade 9's synthesis outward into a position the learner can
defend. This is the year a learner becomes a full co-author of their own mind and of the
shared world — examining identity as constructed across cultures, critiquing rhetoric
and moral argument for hidden assumptions, and meeting the hardest truths of history and
the environment with intellectual tools, never nihilism.

The year opens inward. *Feelings, Identity & Purpose* (U.10.001, D01) continues Grade
9's consolidation, examining emotion and identity through their cultural and historical
roots, and holding a considered position on what defines them independent of labels.
*Body, Wellness & Movement* (U.10.002, D03) reads the body as the somatic ground of
thought and feeling, explaining how different cultures and times have understood the
body–mind connection and assessing what evidence supports each. It builds sustainable
habits and movement, reads fitness technology for what it reveals and distorts, and
weighs what food choices cost the Earth — without shame for any body.
*Relationships, Community & Mutual Aid* (U.10.003, D02) turns outward into care:
de-escalation, cross-cultural empathy from within a community's own history, and the
mutual-aid networks that hold a society together.

The heart of the year is given to the three great tools of school.
*Language, Literature & Argument* (U.10.004, D04) grows from Grade 9's synthesis into
criticism and craft: critiquing rhetoric, reading extended works whole, and shaping
prose with deliberate style — always home language first, with multilingualism held as
wealth.
*Mathematics & Reasoning* (U.10.005, D05) climbs the next rung of modeling and proof:
exponents and the complex plane, polynomial and exponential models, trigonometry, circle
theorems, and the probability that reads everyday risk.
*Science: Systems, Evidence & Our Origins* (U.10.006, D06) carries Grade 9's evidence
discipline into statistics and peer review, genetics and human impacts on ecosystems,
motion and energy, and the Big Bang held as a model, never dogma.

*Creative & Aesthetic Expression* (U.10.007, D07) gives the imagination craft and an
ethical eye, developing a personal style, composing, performing, and designing for a
community need while honoring cultural appreciation over appropriation.
*Health, Sexuality & Safety* (U.10.008, D03) meets the body with the honesty and agency
high school demands, answering questions about sexual health accurately and judging the
sources that claim to know.

The final third steps into the adult questions of power, justice, and the living
Earth. *Global History: Time, Culture & Atrocity* (U.10.009, D08) gives the year its
many-voiced human story — how societies measured time, colonization and migration, and a
case of genocide remembered honestly, never as one nation's triumph.
*Ethics, Justice & Political Economy* (U.10.010, D09) turns that reasoning into
critique and action: rebuilding moral arguments more fairly, weighing responsibility for
climate harm and poverty, and designing collective action, restorative repair, and
peacebuilding. *Technology, Media & Our Earth Home* (U.10.011, D10) gathers the outward
year into stewardship — surveillance and automation, the promises and risks of AI, and
the ecosystem services, tipping points, and circular economies the Earth home depends
on. The year closes in *All of Me, All of Us* (U.10.012, D01 + D02), gathering the
considered position, the caring community, and the living Earth, and looking ahead to
Grade 11.

Throughout, the four pillars are interwoven. Intellectual awareness (P3) carries the
most weight — criticism and craft in language, modeling and proof in mathematics, the
systems-and-evidence discipline of science, and the creative work of the arts — while
contextual and ecological awareness (P4) holds a large, steady share, for Grade 10 faces
the hardest truths of history and the environment. Emotional and social awareness (P1)
remains the ground: identity is examined and defended, and belonging matters more than
ever. A known, cared-for, capable body-and-self (P2) is the foundation all of it stands
on. Science (D06), global history (D08), ethics (D09), and technology and environment
(D10) each carry a full unit and spiral deeper in Grades 11–12.
