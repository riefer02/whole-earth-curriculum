# Unit 7 — Creative & Aesthetic Expression

## Overview

This unit gives craft and an ethical eye to the imagination now that formal reasoning
has matured, building on Grade 9's digital and mixed-media work, music, performance,
and design. Learners develop a personal style by making a body of work and explain
how it reflects their ideas and influences, and distinguish cultural appreciation
from appropriation in art, citing examples and explaining how to honor living
traditions respectfully. They compose and record an original piece that expresses a
clear idea or feeling and explain the techniques used, and distinguish respectful
engagement with a musical tradition from appropriation. They devise and perform an
original piece that develops a clear concept and demonstrates craft in movement and
expression, and distinguish respectful engagement with a dance or dramatic tradition
from appropriation. They design and make something that serves a community need with
attention to sustainability and fairness and document the decisions, and distinguish
responsible and sustainable design from design that harms people or the environment.
The arts of other cultures are met as living traditions to honor with respectful
questions, never as specimens.

## Essential questions

- How do I develop a personal style through a body of work, and how do I tell cultural appreciation from appropriation in art?
- How do I compose, record, devise, and perform original work with craft, and honor living traditions respectfully?
- How do I design and make something that serves a community need with sustainability and fairness, and tell responsible design from harmful design?
