# Unit 3 — Relationships, Community & Mutual Aid

## Overview

This unit turns outward into the practice of care in a connected world, building on
Grade 9's communication-across-difference and forms-of-community work. Learners
evaluate how technology and social media shape communication and use digital tools
respectfully, and handle conflict through de-escalation, seeking mediation when a
conflict is too big to solve alone. They practice cross-cultural empathy by studying
a community's history and values from within, and evaluate how digital platforms
shape empathy, compassion fatigue, and echo chambers. They evaluate how technology
and media create and transform communities and belonging, explain how local
communities connect to regional, national, and global ones, analyze how technology
can enable or undermine cooperation and mutual aid globally, and examine examples of
cooperative economies and mutual-aid networks across the world.

## Essential questions

- How do I use digital tools respectfully, and how do I de-escalate conflict and seek mediation when it is too big to solve alone?
- How do I practice cross-cultural empathy from within a community's own history, and how do digital platforms shape empathy and echo chambers?
- How do local communities connect to the global, and how can technology enable or undermine cooperation and mutual aid?
