# Unit 12 — All of Me, All of Us

## Overview

This unit gathers the year together, building on Grade 9's closing celebration.
Learners return to the considered position on what defines them, independent of
external labels and expectations, and reflect on how they direct their own learning
— choosing goals, resources, and ways to check their progress. They reflect on how
their local community connects to regional, national, and global ones, and examine
examples of cooperative economies and mutual-aid networks across the world as the
shape of care in action. They celebrate each whole person, the caring community they
have built, and the living Earth — and look ahead to Grade 11, where the considered
position becomes an articulated purpose and a meaningful life, empathy extends to
distant and future others, and the ethics of technology and the environment mature
into governance, solidarity, and stewardship across generations.

## Essential questions

- What considered position now defines me, and how do I direct my own learning into Grade 11 and beyond?
- How do I stand with others from my local community to the global one, and how do cooperation and mutual aid carry me forward?
