# Unit 2 — Body, Wellness & Movement

## Overview

This unit reads the body as the somatic ground of thought and feeling and as a system
shaped by habit, building on Grade 9's somatic basis of emotion and its
movement-and-food-system work. Learners analyze how chronic stress, sleep, and
movement shape mood, thinking, and long-term health, and explain how different
cultures and times have understood the body–mind connection, assessing what evidence
supports each. They apply principles of training — progressive effort and recovery —
to build a safe and sustainable movement plan, and evaluate how technology such as
fitness trackers and apps supports or distorts a healthy relationship with movement
and the body. They evaluate popular diets and nutrition claims using scientific
evidence, without shaming any body, and explain the environmental footprint of
different foods and diets and how food choices affect climate and ecosystems. The
body is taught with dignity and joy, honoring the wide range of bodies and abilities.

## Essential questions

- How do chronic stress, sleep, and movement shape my mood, thinking, and long-term health, and how have different cultures understood the body–mind connection?
- How do I build a safe, sustainable movement plan, and how does technology help or distort my relationship with my body?
- How do I evaluate nutrition claims with evidence, without shaming any body, and what is the environmental footprint of what I eat?
