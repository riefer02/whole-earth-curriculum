# Unit 8 — Health, Sexuality & Safety

## Overview

This unit returns to the body at a turning point, now with the honesty and agency
high school demands, building on Grade 9's sexual and reproductive health and its
help-seeking work. Learners analyze the risks and responsibilities of sexual
activity, including contraception and preventing infections, using accurate
information, and evaluate sources of health information — online, peer, and marketing
— for accuracy and bias. Every body, every pace, and every family's way of living is
honored; the body is taught with dignity, never shame, and questions are met with
accurate information and care, not fear.

## Essential questions

- How do I analyze the risks and responsibilities of sexual activity, including contraception and preventing infections, with accurate information?
- How do I evaluate sources of health information — online, peer, and marketing — for accuracy and bias?
