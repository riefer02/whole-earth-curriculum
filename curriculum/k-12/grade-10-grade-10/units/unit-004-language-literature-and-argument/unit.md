# Unit 4 — Language, Literature & Argument

## Overview

This unit grows from Grade 9's synthesis and authorship into *criticism and craft*,
building on the analysis of structure, style, and argument and the writing of
research-supported arguments. Learners critique rhetoric in public speech and media,
distinguishing emotional appeal from reasoned argument, and speak to a public or
unfamiliar audience with confidence, adapting register and formality to the setting.
They critically evaluate digital and print media for bias, omission, and the
difference between evidence and belief, and read extended, complex literary and
informational texts, tracking themes and arguments across a full work. They craft
prose with deliberate style, varying sentence structure, word choice, and tone to
suit purpose and audience, and compose multimodal texts that combine writing, image,
sound, and design with attention to accessibility. They analyze how language and
power interact — whose language is used in law, medicine, and school — and
communicate respectfully in a multicultural setting, adapting to different cultural
norms of politeness and address. Home language is honored first; multilingualism is
held as wealth.

## Essential questions

- How do I critique rhetoric, distinguishing emotional appeal from reasoned argument, and speak with confidence to an unfamiliar audience?
- How do I read extended, complex works and evaluate media for bias, omission, and the difference between evidence and belief?
- How do I craft prose and accessible multimodal texts with deliberate style, and how does language and power interact across cultures?
