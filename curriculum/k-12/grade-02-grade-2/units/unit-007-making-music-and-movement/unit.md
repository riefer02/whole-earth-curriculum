# Unit 7 — Making, Music & Movement

## Overview

This unit gives the whole body and the imagination to making, song, and movement,
building on Grade 1's lines, colors, beat, and pretend. Learners mix two colors to
make a new one, compare two artworks, play or sing a short rhythm or melody pattern
and repeat it accurately, identify an instrument by its sound and the mood of the
music, create and repeat a short movement sequence, act out a scene with a partner
taking turns leading and following, plan a making task by choosing materials and steps
before starting, and compare two ways to solve a making problem. Every culture's arts
are treated as a living heritage.

## Essential questions

- How do I make art, music, and movement that say something?
- How do I plan what I will make and choose between ways to do it?
