# Unit 3 — Caring & Working Together

## Overview

This unit turns the year outward into relationship, building on Grade 1's kind words
and taking turns and Kindergarten's sharing. Learners give sincere compliments and
encouragement, resolve a simple disagreement with words, describe how two people in
the same situation might feel differently, show caring when a classmate is sad, left
out, or hurt, explain what makes a group welcoming and inclusive, describe a community
tradition, cooperate to finish a task no one could do alone, and explain why helping
others helps everyone. Relationship is taught as skills to practice in a spirit of
mutual aid, not rules to obey.

## Essential questions

- How do I care for others and work with them to do what I cannot do alone?
- What makes a group welcoming, and how do communities help one another?
