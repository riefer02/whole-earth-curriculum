# Unit 11 — Tools, Media & Our Earth Home

## Overview

This unit turns to the tools, media, and living Earth we share, building on Grade 1's
tools and Kindergarten's caring for the Earth. Learners explain that tools and machines
are made by people to solve a problem, compare an old tool and a new tool for the same
job, tell where a piece of information came from and ask a question about it, tell
whether a message is giving true information or selling something, describe a habitat
and the plants and animals that live there, explain that animals get energy from
eating plants or other animals, name a way people use water or energy and a way to
save some of it, and follow what happens to litter. Technology and media are taught
with critical judgment, and ecology as the ground we stand on.

## Essential questions

- How do tools help us, and how do I tell true information from a sales message?
- How do living things share a habitat, and how can I care for water, energy, and the land?
