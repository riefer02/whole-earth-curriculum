# Unit 9 — People, Places & Long Ago

## Overview

This unit steps into the wider human story, building on Grade 1's sequencing of a day
and Kindergarten's "then and now." Learners order events from their family's or
community's recent past on a simple timeline, describe how one part of daily life has
changed over time and one way it has stayed the same, describe the daily life of
children in another place or time, identify a knowledge or skill a people has
developed, explain what people need from their environment and how people in different
places meet those needs, describe how people adapt to a very different environment,
and identify fair and unfair ways of treating people and a story of someone who helped
or spoke up. History is told from many perspectives, never a single nation's triumph.

## Essential questions

- How do I tell the story of time on a timeline — what came before and what changed?
- How do people live in different places and meet their needs?
