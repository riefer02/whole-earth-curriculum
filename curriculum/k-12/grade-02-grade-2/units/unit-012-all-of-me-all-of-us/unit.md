# Unit 12 — All of Me, All of Us

## Overview

The year closes by gathering all of it together. Learners revisit and celebrate their
feelings and strengths, the ways they are alike and different, the calming strategies
they now choose on their own, the welcoming community they have helped build, and the
care they have practiced all year. Through a portfolio celebration — sharing writing,
number work, predictions and made things, and a remembered act of care — each child
sees themselves as a whole person and sees their class as a caring community to which
they belong. This unit spirals back to the year's opening and sends the learner forward
into Grade 3.

## Essential questions

- Who have I become this year, and who are we together?
- How do I celebrate myself and my community?
