# Unit 2 — My Body, My Senses & Movement

## Overview

This unit returns to the body, building on Grade 1's naming of body parts and
Kindergarten's five senses. Learners match each sense to the body part that does the
sensing and describe what each sense tells us, notice where a feeling shows up in the
body (a fast heartbeat when excited), combine movements into a sequence, and throw,
catch, and kick a ball with developing control using both hands and both feet. The
body is honored with dignity and joy, and the wide range of bodies and abilities is
welcomed.

## Essential questions

- How do my senses tell me about the world, and how does my body tell me how I feel?
- How can I move my body with control, alone and with others?
