# Lesson 9 — Throw, Catch, and Kick Together

## Summary

Learners bring their three ball skills together and share them. In pairs and small
games they throw, catch, and kick a soft ball to a friend and into a goal, learning
that a ball is more fun — and a friend more trusted — when you pass and take turns.

## Objectives

- Throw, catch, and kick a ball with developing control using both hands and both
  feet. _(D03.S2.02.02)_

## Connection

A ball by yourself is a ball; a ball with a friend is a game. In every place people
live, passing a ball back and forth is how friends play together. Passing takes two
skills at once: your hands or feet do the work, and your kindness waits for the other
person's turn.

## Materials

- Soft balls or beanbags
- Floor markers to mark where partners stand and the goal
- A goal (cones, a box, or chalk lines)

## Preparation

- Mark partner spots facing each other across a safe gap, and a goal at one end.
- Set out soft balls and beanbags.
- Clear the space so passes and kicks travel safely.

## Facilitator note

This lesson is written to the learner ("you") so it works in a classroom and for a
self-directed learner. Prepare the partner spots and goal, then model a gentle pass and
a kick before pairs try. The heart of this lesson is cooperation: celebrate passing,
waiting, and encouraging, not winning or scoring the most. Honor every body: pairs can
pass seated, with wheels, or with a scoop or larger ball, and every version counts
fully. Rotate partners so learners play with different friends. Watch for a learner
who always gives the ball away and never gets it back — gently model taking turns so
everyone both gives and receives.

## Procedure

1. **Gather (5 min).** You can throw, catch, and kick. Today you will share all three
   with a friend. A ball is more fun when you pass it.
2. **Pass back and forth (15 min).** Stand or sit facing your partner. Take turns:
   throw the ball with both hands, your partner catches with both hands, then they
   throw it back. Try a few gentle passes. If the ball drops, no problem — pick it up
   and try again.
3. **Kick to a goal (10 min).** Now take turns kicking. One of you kicks the ball
   gently toward the goal, the other stops it and kicks it back. Try with your left
   foot, then your right.
4. **Play a small passing game (10 min).** Put it together: throw, catch, kick —
   pass to your partner, they catch, they kick toward the goal, you bring it back. Say
   kind words as you play: "Nice pass!" "Your turn!"
5. **Close (5 min).** How did it feel to share the ball? A game with a friend is
   built on turns and kind words, just as much as on hands and feet.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Keep the gap small, use a large soft ball, and let a learner choose to
  roll instead of throw or kick if that is more comfortable.
- **Extension:** Ask pairs to pass with a "one bounce" catch or to kick through a
  narrower goal, still taking turns.

## Assessment

- **Formative (observation/peer):** Can the learner throw, catch, and kick a soft ball
  to a partner with growing control, and take turns with kind words?

## Home connection

Play a passing game at home with a soft ball or rolled-up socks — throw, catch, and
kick it back and forth, taking turns.
