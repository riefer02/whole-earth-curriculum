# Unit 6 — Wondering & Testing: The Living World

## Overview

This unit turns wonder into prediction and test, building on Grade 1's five-senses
observation and Kindergarten's "why?" questions. Learners make a prediction, test it
by doing, and tell whether the result matched; sort and group objects by features;
describe the life cycle of a plant or animal in order; observe and describe a habitat;
sort materials by properties and explain how those properties make them useful; notice
that sound, light, and heat are forms of energy; describe how the Sun warms the Earth
and gives light; and describe rocks, soil, and water as the land we live on. Questions
are held as questions, and evidence is distinguished from belief.

## Essential questions

- How do I predict, test, and see whether my guess was right?
- What do living things need, and how do the Sun and the land help them?
