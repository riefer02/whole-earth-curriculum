# Unit 10 — Fairness & Our Shared Rules

## Overview

This unit turns to ethics and shared life, building on Grade 1's kind and unkind
choices and Kindergarten's sharing and taking turns. Learners tell the difference
between what is right and wrong to do and give a reason, explain how a kind or unkind
act affects another person's feelings, explain why treating everyone the same is not
always fair, name a right children have and why it matters, explain how a group makes
a fair decision together, identify how people in their community help one another and
how they could join in, describe a conflict and a peaceful way to solve it, and explain
what makes an apology real. Questions are held as questions, not answered as dogma.

## Essential questions

- How do I tell right from wrong and think about what is fair?
- How do we decide together, and how do we make things right after a conflict?
