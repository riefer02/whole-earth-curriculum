# Unit 4 — Reading, Writing & Many Languages

## Overview

This unit continues formal literacy, building on Grade 1's decoding and simple
sentences. Learners listen to a partner and retell in order, take turns in
conversation and stay on topic, read common sight words and regularly spelled words
accurately and with growing fluency, answer who, what, where, when, and why questions
about a text, write a short narrative with first, next, and last, write a simple
informative or opinion piece with a reason, share something about their home language
and culture, and recognize that people speak many languages and none is better than
another. The home language is honored first and multilingualism is treated as wealth.

## Essential questions

- How do I read with growing ease and understand what I read?
- How do I share my ideas in writing, in my own language and others'?
