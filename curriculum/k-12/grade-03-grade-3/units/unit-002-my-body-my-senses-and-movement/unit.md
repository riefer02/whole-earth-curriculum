# Unit 2 — My Body, My Senses & Movement

## Overview

This unit returns to the body, building on Grade 2's matching of each sense to the
body part that senses and noticing where a feeling shows up in the body. Learners now
describe how two senses can work together, such as taste and smell, to give a fuller
picture of something; recognize the early body signals of hunger, fullness, thirst,
and tiredness and name one helpful response to each; perform a short movement sequence
such as a dance or obstacle course with rhythm and balance; and improve hand-eye
coordination through games of tossing, catching, and aiming at a target. The body is
honored with dignity and joy, and the wide range of bodies and abilities is welcomed.
Where tools appear — a camera, a tablet, a music player — learners notice what a
machine can do (record or replay a sound, a picture, or a beat) and what only their own
living body can do (sense, signal, and move), and they begin to see that tools are made
somewhere and need power, while the body makes its own beat and awareness with nothing
but itself.

## Essential questions

- How do my senses work together to tell me about the world and my own body?
- How does my body move with rhythm, balance, and control?
