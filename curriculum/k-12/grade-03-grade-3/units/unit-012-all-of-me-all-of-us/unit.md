# Unit 12 — All of Me, All of Us

## Overview

The year closes by gathering all of it together. Learners revisit and celebrate their
growing understanding of mixed feelings and the story of their own life, the groups
that shape them and the goal they set, the welcoming and fairly-run community they
have helped build, and the care they have practiced all year. Through a portfolio
celebration — sharing writing, number work, fair tests and made things, and a
remembered act of care — each child sees themselves as a whole person and sees their
class as a caring community to which they belong. This unit spirals back to the year's
opening and sends the learner forward into Grade 4.

## Essential questions

- Who have I become this year, and who are we together?
- How do I celebrate myself and my community, and look ahead?
