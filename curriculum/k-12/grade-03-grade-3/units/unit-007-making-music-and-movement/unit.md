# Unit 7 — Making, Music & Movement

## Overview

This unit gives the whole body and the imagination to making, building on Grade 2's
color mixing, rhythm patterns, and movement sequences. Learners use a simple pattern
or repetition of shape, line, or color in an artwork and describe the mood of an
artwork and what in the picture creates it; create and perform a short rhythm pattern
using long and short sounds and describe how changing tempo, dynamics, or pitch
changes a song's feeling; perform a short dance or movement piece that uses high and
low levels, speeds, and shapes, and retell a story or feeling using only movement and
gesture while explaining their choices; and follow a simple design process of imagine,
plan, make, and improve to build something that meets a need, explaining how the
materials available shape what can be made. Every culture's arts are treated as a
living heritage, not a museum piece.

## Essential questions

- How do I use pattern, sound, and movement to express an idea or feeling?
- How does a design process help me make something that works?
