# Unit 10 — Fairness & Our Shared Rules

## Overview

This unit deepens fairness and civic life, building on Grade 2's telling right from
wrong and understanding that same treatment is not always fair. Learners give reasons
for and against a simple choice and say what they would do and why; explain why the
same act can be good in one situation and not in another; tell the difference between
a need and a want and why meeting needs fairly matters; identify a situation where
someone was left out or treated unequally and describe how to make it more fair;
explain how rules keep a group safe and fair and propose a rule for a shared space
with a reason; explain what it means to have a voice in a group and what can happen
when someone's voice is not heard; use a simple step-by-step way to calm down and talk
through a conflict without hurting anyone; and explain what it means to repair harm
rather than only punish. Questions are held as questions, not answered as dogma.

## Essential questions

- How do I weigh reasons and tell needs from wants to decide what is fair?
- How do rules, voices, and repair help a community live fairly together?
