# Unit 4 — Reading, Writing & Many Languages

## Overview

This unit continues formal literacy, building on Grade 2's fluency, retelling, and
first short pieces of writing. Learners read grade-level texts with accuracy and
fluency, self-correcting when meaning breaks down; identify the main idea and
supporting details of a short informational or narrative text; retell a spoken story
or explanation in sequence with key details; ask and answer questions to understand a
speaker's meaning and explain one idea to a small group; write a paragraph with a
topic sentence, at least two supporting details, and a closing sentence; revise and
edit their own writing for spelling, punctuation, and clarity; learn and use simple
everyday phrases in another language; and notice that the same idea can be said in
many languages. The learner's home language is honored first, and multilingualism is
treated as wealth, not deficit.

## Essential questions

- How do I read for meaning and find the main idea?
- How do I write and improve a clear paragraph, and share ideas across languages?
