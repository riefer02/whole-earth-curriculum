# Unit 11 — Tools, Media & Our Earth Home

## Overview

This unit meets the tools, media, and living Earth we are stewards of, building on
Grade 2's tools-that-solve-problems, telling information from selling, and caring for
places. Learners name a simple machine, such as a lever, wheel, or ramp, and tell how
it makes a job easier; describe how one tool or machine changed the way a job was done
long ago compared with today; tell the difference between an advertisement and
information and give one clue that something is an ad; explain that a message has a
purpose and an audience and name both for a simple message; build or draw a simple
food chain with a plant and an animal and show the direction energy moves; explain
that green plants make their own food using sunlight and that all living things depend
on plants or on animals that eat them; tell the difference between a natural resource
that can run out and one that comes back; and name one resource people use every day,
where it comes from, and one way to use less of it. Technology is taught with critical
judgment, neither worship nor fear.

## Essential questions

- How do tools and media work, and how can I tell what a message is for?
- How does energy move through living things, and how do we care for Earth's resources?
