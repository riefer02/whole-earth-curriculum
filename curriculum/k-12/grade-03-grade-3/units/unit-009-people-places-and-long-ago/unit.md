# Unit 9 — People, Places & Long Ago

## Overview

This unit steps into the wider human story, building on Grade 2's simple timelines and
then-and-now stories. Learners place local events or landmarks on a timeline in order
and explain how they know the order is correct; describe how a place or community
changed over a long time and what caused one of those changes; compare how two peoples
met basic needs for food, shelter, and clothing and explain how their environment
shaped those choices; describe a story, song, or art form from a culture and what it
shows about what that culture values; describe reasons people move from one place to
another; explain how rivers, mountains, or climate shaped where people chose to settle;
and describe a story of people working together to change something unfair, and what
it means to have power or a say in a group. History is told from many perspectives,
honoring the voices too often left out.

## Essential questions

- How do places and people change over time, and what causes change?
- How do people's needs, movement, and voices shape the human story?
