# Unit 5 — Numbers, Shapes, Patterns & Data

## Overview

This unit continues formal numeracy, building on Grade 2's place value of tens and
ones and adding and subtracting within 100. Learners use place value to 1,000 to read,
write, round, and compare whole numbers; multiply and divide within 100 and explain
the meaning of each operation using objects, drawings, and known facts; identify
arithmetic patterns in tables and number grids and explain them using the properties
of operations; solve two-step word problems using the four operations and explain the
relationship between addition and subtraction or multiplication and division;
understand the area and perimeter of rectangles and solve problems with them in real
contexts; sort shapes into categories by their attributes; measure and estimate liquid
volume and mass using standard units; and draw scaled picture and bar graphs to solve
problems and make comparisons. Mathematics is taught as sense-making with real things,
drawn from many traditions, for everyone.

## Essential questions

- How do multiplication and division help me understand equal groups?
- How do I measure, shape, and read data to answer real questions?
