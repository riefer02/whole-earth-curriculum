# Unit 3 — Caring, Perspective & Belonging

## Overview

This unit turns outward into the practice of care, building on Grade 2's giving
encouragement, resolving disagreements with words, and cooperating on a shared task.
Learners now take turns speaking in a group discussion and build on what a classmate
said, use "I" statements to say how they feel without blaming during a conflict,
retell a story from another character's point of view, imagine and describe what it is
like to be someone else in a familiar place, compare their own community with a
community in a different place or culture, explain how rules and routines help a
community work fairly for everyone, practice contributing fairly to a group goal, and
give examples of how people in a community help one another. Care is taught as mutual
aid, not competition.

## Essential questions

- How do I listen to, and see through, another person's eyes?
- How do communities work fairly, and how do I help?
