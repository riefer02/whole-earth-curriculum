# Unit 1 — Mixed Feelings, My Story & My Goals

## Overview

This unit opens the year inward, building on Grade 2's work of telling similar
feelings apart and noticing where a feeling lives in the body. Learners now describe
how a feeling can change over time and name when they feel more than one feeling at
once, explain how the same situation can make different people feel differently, tell
the story of their own life so far in order with its important people and places, name
the several groups they belong to and how each one shapes who they are, set a simple
personal goal and name its first step, and practice managing a strong feeling in a
group without disrupting others. Every family's shape and every child's pace are
honored.

## Essential questions

- How do feelings change over time, and what does it mean to feel more than one thing at once?
- Who am I becoming, and what is my next step?
