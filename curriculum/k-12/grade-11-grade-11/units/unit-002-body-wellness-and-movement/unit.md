# Unit 2 — Body, Wellness & Movement

## Overview

This unit reads the body as the somatic ground of thought and feeling, now practiced
deliberately and understood as a shared public concern, building on Grade 10's
body-as-system and evidence-evaluated nutrition. Learners use mind-body practices
such as breathing, movement, and mindfulness deliberately to regulate their own state
and explain why they work, and analyze how disability, illness, and diverse bodies
shape experience, designing practices that honor a wide range of bodies. They compare
movement traditions across cultures — yoga, martial arts, dance, and sport — and what
each offers for health and community, and design inclusive movement activities that
adapt to a wide range of bodies, abilities, and environments. They analyze how public
policy and food systems shape population nutrition and health and propose a change
that widens access, and build a sustainable personal nutrition plan that honors their
own needs, culture, budget, and values. The body is taught with dignity and joy,
honoring the wide range of bodies and abilities.

## Essential questions

- How do I use mind-body practices such as breathing, movement, and mindfulness deliberately to regulate my state, and why do they work?
- How do disability, illness, and diverse bodies shape experience, and how do I design movement and practices that honor a wide range of bodies?
- How do public policy and food systems shape population nutrition and health, and how do I build a sustainable nutrition plan that honors my needs, culture, budget, and values?
