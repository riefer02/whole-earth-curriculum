# Unit 3 — Relationships, Community & Solidarity

## Overview

This unit turns outward into the practice of care at its most mature — across
difference and distance — building on Grade 10's connected-world relationships and
mutual aid. Learners practice deep listening across disagreement, staying open to
being changed by what others say, and build and maintain close, trusting
relationships while offering the same support to others. They apply empathy to people
they have never met, far away or in future generations, and weigh empathy for
individuals against fairness to a group when the two conflict. They analyze what
makes communities resilient, inclusive, and just, using historical and contemporary
examples, and envision and plan a contribution that strengthens belonging in a
community they care about. They argue for a position on care, solidarity, and
collective responsibility toward distant others, and plan and begin a sustained
mutual-aid or cooperative effort with accountability.

## Essential questions

- How do I practice deep listening across disagreement, staying open to being changed, while building and maintaining close, trusting relationships?
- How do I apply empathy to people I have never met, and weigh empathy for individuals against fairness to a group?
- What makes communities resilient, inclusive, and just, and how do I plan a contribution and a sustained mutual-aid or cooperative effort with accountability?
