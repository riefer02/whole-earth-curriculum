# Unit 1 — Feelings, Identity & Purpose

## Overview

This unit opens the year inward, building on Grade 10's examined-and-defended
identity. Learners now *articulate and commit* to it: they articulate the emotional
climate of a group or society and how collective emotions such as hope, fear, and
grief arise, and reflect on their own emotional development over time, naming areas
of strength and growth. They articulate their developing values, purpose, and sense
of what a meaningful life looks like, and reflect on their place in a global,
interconnected world and what they owe to people unlike themselves. They make
decisions that align their actions with their values even under pressure or
uncertainty, and take authorship of a personal project or commitment, seeing it
through with self-direction. Every self is honored as a whole in the making, never a
label, and every question is held as a question.

## Essential questions

- How do collective emotions such as hope, fear, and grief arise in a group or society, and how do I read and shape that climate?
- What are my developing values, purpose, and sense of a meaningful life, and what do I owe to people unlike myself in a global world?
- How do I make decisions that align my actions with my values under pressure, and see a personal commitment through with self-direction?
