# Unit 8 — Health, Care & Access

## Overview

This unit returns to the body at a turning point, now with the agency and
systems-awareness high school demands, building on Grade 10's honest sexuality and
health-information evaluation. Learners plan for their own health-care needs,
including how to access care, advocate for themselves, and navigate health systems,
and explain how chronic conditions and disabilities affect daily life and design
respectful support for themselves and others. Every body, every pace, and every
family's way of living is honored; the body is taught with dignity, never shame, and
questions are met with accurate information and care, not fear.

## Essential questions

- How do I plan for my own health-care needs, including how to access care, advocate for myself, and navigate health systems?
- How do chronic conditions and disabilities affect daily life, and how do I design respectful support for myself and others?
