# Unit 7 — Creative & Aesthetic Expression

## Overview

This unit gives the co-author's deliberate, ethical voice to the imagination, building
on Grade 10's craft and appreciation-versus-appropriation ethics. Learners create an
artwork that addresses a social or environmental issue and explain its intended
effect on an audience, and curate a small exhibition of artworks, their own or
others', writing the reasoning behind the selection and order. They arrange or
compose a piece that responds to a social or environmental issue and explain its
intended effect, and curate a listening program of works from several cultures,
writing the reasoning behind the selection. They create a performance that responds
to a social or environmental issue and explain its intended effect on an audience,
and direct or curate a short performance program, writing the reasoning behind the
selection and staging. They design a solution to a social or environmental problem,
building and testing a prototype and explaining its intended impact, and evaluate the
full life cycle of a designed object, from materials and making through use and
disposal, proposing improvements. The arts of other cultures are met as living
traditions to honor with respectful questions, never as specimens.

## Essential questions

- How do I create artwork, music, and performance that respond to a social or environmental issue and move an audience?
- How do I curate exhibitions, listening programs, and performances, and write the reasoning behind my selection and order?
- How do I design a solution with a tested prototype, and evaluate the full life cycle of a designed object from materials to disposal?
