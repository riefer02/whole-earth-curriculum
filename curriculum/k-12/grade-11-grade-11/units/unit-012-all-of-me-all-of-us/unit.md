# Unit 12 — All of Me, All of Us

## Overview

This unit gathers the year together, building on Grade 10's closing celebration.
Learners return to their developing values, purpose, and sense of what a meaningful
life looks like, and reflect on how they take authorship of a personal project or
commitment, seeing it through with self-direction. They reflect on envisioning and
planning a contribution that strengthens belonging in a community they care about,
and on planning and beginning a sustained mutual-aid or cooperative effort with
accountability. They celebrate each whole person, the solidary community they have
built, and the living Earth — and look ahead to Grade 12, the capstone year of
integration and life purpose, where the articulated purpose becomes an integrated
sense of who they are and how they will act in the world.

## Essential questions

- What are my developing values, purpose, and sense of a meaningful life, and how do I take authorship of a personal project into Grade 12 and beyond?
- How do I envision and plan a contribution that strengthens belonging, and begin a sustained mutual-aid or cooperative effort with accountability?
