# Vision

> The North Star. Everything in this repository exists to serve this document.
> If a change does not serve this vision, it does not belong.

## The North Star

To raise the baseline standard of living for **all life on Earth** — from the
cellular level to the anthropological level, and for the entire Earth environment —
by educating humans who are **emotionally, physically, and intellectually aware** of
themselves, the context they live in, and the environment around them.

## The problem

Public education in many nations — including, but not limited to, the United States —
is failing. It often:

- Fragments the human into disconnected subjects instead of developing a whole person.
- Prepares students to fit into existing hierarchies rather than to build a more just
  one.
- Centers a narrow, often nationalist or Eurocentric, view of history and culture.
- Treats ethics, emotion, and the body as afterthoughts to "academic" content.
- Ignores or reacts to contemporary technology rather than teaching its effects on
  humanity and the environment.

WEC exists to be a **drop-in replacement** — a complete, coherent K–12 curriculum
that a school, a community, a family, or a self-directed learner can adopt in whole
or in part.

## The goal

Produce humans who are:

1. **Emotionally aware** — of their own inner life and that of others; capable of
   empathy, self-regulation, and healthy relationship.
2. **Physically aware** — of their body, health, movement, and the somatic basis of
   feeling and thought.
3. **Intellectually aware** — of how to think, reason, and know; critical thinkers
   who distinguish evidence from belief and who learn for life, not for tests.
4. **Contextually and ecologically aware** — of the societies, histories, and natural
   systems they live within, and of their responsibility to them.

## The four pillars

The curriculum is organized under four pillars. Every domain, strand, standard, unit,
and lesson descends from one or more of them.

| ID | Pillar | Question it answers |
|----|--------|---------------------|
| **P1** | Emotional & Social Awareness | Who am I, and how do I live well with others? |
| **P2** | Physical & Somatic Awareness | How do I understand and care for my body? |
| **P3** | Intellectual & Cognitive Awareness | How do I think, reason, and know? |
| **P4** | Contextual & Ecological Awareness | What world do I live in, and what do I owe it? |

These pillars are **not subjects**; they are dimensions of a whole person. A single
lesson may serve several at once, and the curriculum deliberately interweaves them.

## Values that bind us

- **Egalitarian.** The curriculum is designed to benefit the majority, not a select
  few. It explicitly rejects hierarchy, supremacy, and the enrichment of the few.
- **Holistic.** Mind, body, emotion, and context develop together, in spiral, from
  Kindergarten through Grade 12 and beyond.
- **Humanitarian.** The measure of success is human (and more-than-human) flourishing,
  not test scores or economic output.
- **Global & anthropological.** History, culture, and knowledge are taught from a
  global, cross-cultural, and scientific perspective — never from a single nation's
  default.
- **Ethical.** Ethics and morality are not a single unit; they are a lens through
  which all content is taught, and a discipline of their own.
- **Honest about technology.** Contemporary technologies and their effects on
  humanity and the environment are a core subject, taught with neither worship nor
  fear, but with critical judgment.
- **Open.** The curriculum is offered to the world for free, at no cost, and remains
  open through its license.

## How we stay aligned

This vision is not prose we write once and forget. It is enforced by the
**traceability chain**:

```
Vision → Pillar → Domain → Strand → Standard → Unit → Lesson
```

Every lesson declares which standards it serves; every standard declares its strand,
domain, and pillar. An automated validator and dedicated alignment agents keep the
chain intact. When in doubt about any content, return here and ask: *does this raise
the baseline of awareness and flourishing for all life?*
