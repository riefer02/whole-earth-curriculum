# Transparent budget and measurement plan

Whole Earth Curriculum will publish costs against concrete outcomes rather than
announce a polished total before the work is measured. This document is the public
baseline. It will be updated when funding is received or paid work begins.

## What every funded outcome will show

- The deliverable and who it is meant to serve.
- The estimate, currency, rates, hours, quantities, and assumptions behind it.
- Funding received, spending by category, remaining balance, and material in-kind
  support.
- The evidence produced, limitations found, revisions made, and publication status.
- Any variance from the estimate and what caused it.

No donor or partner receives private ownership of the curriculum. Curriculum remains
CC BY-SA 4.0 and tooling remains MIT licensed.

## Initial cost models

| Outcome | Estimate model | Evidence published |
|---|---|---|
| Human review | Lessons × review hours per lesson × reviewer rate | Discipline, time, findings, revisions, and sign-off status |
| Classroom pilot | Educator support or stipend + coordination + analysis | Setting, lessons used, preparation time, barriers, feedback, and resulting changes |
| Translation and localization | Source words × translation rate + cultural and subject review | Language, locale, word count, reviewers, revisions, and publication status |
| Print, offline, and web delivery | Scoped implementation hours + production and service costs | Deliverables, accessibility checks, format, reach, and recurring costs |
| Coordination and administration | Named hours or an explicit percentage | Work performed, spending by category, balance, and variance |

## Outcomes before claims

The repository currently demonstrates scope, traceability, automated validation, and
public access. It does not yet demonstrate classroom effectiveness, adoption, or a
stable per-learner cost. The first pilot cohort and independent reviews are intended
to establish those baselines. Reports will describe the settings and limitations so
results are not generalized beyond the evidence.

## Publication cadence

Before funded work starts, WEC will publish its estimate and assumptions. During
active work, material scope or budget changes will be recorded. At completion, WEC
will publish actual spending and the resulting deliverables or findings. Personal
contact information and confidential partner information will not be published.

Until the first funded outcome begins, the honest public total is: no outcome budget
has been established and no project funding is represented here as received.
