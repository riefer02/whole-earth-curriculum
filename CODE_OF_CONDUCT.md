# Code of Conduct

Whole Earth Curriculum is a humanitarian project: it exists to benefit all life.
Our collaboration must reflect the values we teach.

## Our standards

- **Be respectful and kind.** Every contributor is a human with equal dignity.
- **Assume good faith.** Interpret generously; disagree constructively.
- **Center the mission.** This project serves students, communities, and the planet —
  not ego, prestige, or profit.
- **Include everyone.** Global, cross-cultural perspectives are not an edge case;
  they are the point. Avoid U.S.-centric, Eurocentric, or otherwise narrow framing
  unless the curriculum explicitly calls for it.
- **Ground claims in evidence.** For factual and scientific content, cite sources.
  Distinguish clearly between evidence, theory, and values.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or the introduction of content that
promotes hierarchy, supremacy, or the enrichment of a select few over the many.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may remove contributions that do not align with the project's mission. If you
experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers.
